:::read ON THE BOARD -- SLIPPED UNDER DOORS, NOT POSTED
The Dean's election is counted at midnight. The ballots locked in the Old Deanery are already filled in.

We have forty honest ones, boxed, and no way past a locked room -- so we need people who are good at rooms.

Swap the box. Don't burn it, don't take it, swap it, so that nobody ever knows it happened. If you're caught we've never met.
:::

:::box plain | HANDOUT -- PEN & INK -- IMPRESSION SHEET
A page from the registry of authorised seals: eleven circular stamp impressions in a ruled grid, each at full size, legend round the rim, name and date printed beneath. Draw them as impressions, not designs -- uneven inking, one struck off-centre, recesses solid black, a burr where the die has worn. The precision of the grid against the sloppiness of the strikes is the whole effect. The eleventh has no name under it, and its legend is in a script the compiler plainly could not read and has copied stroke by stroke, badly. Printed note: *In use. Origin not established.*
:::

:::secrets
1. The Auditor does not care who you are. It cares whether the paperwork is in order, and it will check.
2. A swap that is correctly filed is a swap the Auditor will stamp for you, and hold the door while you carry it.
3. The forms are in the Vault. So is the pen. So is the queue, and the queue is the expensive part.
4. Sam is on Wren's payroll and expects you caught holding the box, at about eleven, in front of the porter.
5. Every alarm level costs a minute outside. Alarm 5 costs you midnight outright.
6. Time runs slow inside the Citadel and the clock on the landing is not what is keeping it.
7. The eleventh seal countersigned all forty ballots and nobody in Registry can read its legend.
8. The office door has never been forced in three hundred years and does not need to be. It opens for correct paperwork.
:::

Two floors of one building and a third that is not in it. **Above the line:** the landing and the office, four minutes apart on the porter's round. **Below it:** the Citadel, entered through the clock case, where an hour costs a minute outside.

:::rooms | AREA | WHAT IS THERE
1 | The landing | The clock, the register of authorised seals on the table, and four minutes between the porter's passes. He tries the office door every time.
2 | The Citadel | Inside the case. Brass, freezing, and much larger than the landing it stands on. Time runs slow. Every corridor is signposted. **Gargoyles at alarm 1, 3 and 5.**
3 | The Vault | Where the seals and stamps actually live. The forms are here. So is the pen. **So is the queue,** and the queue is what costs you midnight.
4 | The office | You come out at ankle height, onto the Dean's carpet, four feet from the box. Forty ballots, stamped and countersigned already, by the eleventh seal.
:::

:::puzzle The Correct Form | the Vault · a heist won at a counter
LOOKS: A queue, a pen on a chain, and a rack of forms, one of which is a Substitution of Sealed Material.
SOLVE: Fill it, stamp it with anything from the rack, and join the queue. The queue is the cost -- it takes real minutes and midnight is a real deadline.
FAIL: Never a dead end. A wrong form still stamps; it just files the swap under the wrong department, and somebody will read it in a fortnight.
:::

:::item The Eleventh Seal | Wondrous, rare -- the Vault, third shelf, unlabelled
Legend in a script nobody in Registry can read, in use, origin not established. It countersigns anything, and everything it countersigns is accepted by every office in the university, including the ones that do not exist. Registry will want it back. Registry does not know it is gone.
:::

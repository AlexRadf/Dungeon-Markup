:::pitch
The Dean's election is counted at midnight, and the ballots locked in the Old Deanery have already been filled in, stamped, and countersigned. The Banned Student Union has forty honest ones in a box and no way into a locked room. There is, however, a grandfather clock on the landing with the pendulum taken out.
!! THEME -- the building has rules, and if you read them properly the rules are a door.
:::

:::read WHERE IT STARTS -- READ IT ON THE LANDING, PORTER TWO FLOORS DOWN
The Dean's office is locked, watched and empty, and there is no way into it. There is a grandfather clock on the landing with a case big enough to stand in, which has not run in a decade, and the pendulum is already gone. Somebody took it out. Recently.
:::

Two floors of one building, and a third that is not in it. **Above the line:** the landing and the office, four minutes apart on the porter's round. **Below:** the Citadel, entered through the clock case, where an hour costs a minute outside.

:::rooms | AREA | WHAT IS THERE
1 | The landing | The clock, the register of authorised seals on the table, and four minutes between the porter's passes. He tries the office door every single time.
2 | The Citadel | Inside the case. Brass, freezing, signposted, and far larger than the landing it stands on. **Gargoyles at alarm 1, 3 and 5.**
3 | The Vault | Where the seals and stamps actually live. The forms are here. So is the pen. **So is the queue**, and the queue is what costs you midnight.
4 | The office | You come out at ankle height, on the Dean's carpet, four feet from the box of forty already-marked ballots.
:::

:::secrets
1. The Auditor does not care who you are. It cares whether the paperwork is in order, and it will read it.
2. A swap that is correctly filed is a swap the Auditor will stamp for you and hold the door while you carry.
3. The forms are in the Vault. So is the pen. So is the queue, and the queue is the expensive part.
4. Sam is on Wren's payroll and expects you caught holding the box at about eleven, in front of the porter.
5. Time runs slow inside the Citadel, and the clock on the landing is definitely not what is keeping it.
6. The eleventh seal countersigned all forty ballots and nobody in Registry can read its legend.
:::

:::cols 2
:::box red | TROUBLE
- Alarm 1 to 5, each level putting more gargoyles on the floor and a minute off the clock.
- The Auditor, which cannot be fought at these levels and does not need to be.
- Time runs slow inside the Citadel. A gift, until somebody works out what it costs.
- You are carrying a box. Everything is harder while you are carrying a box.
:::

:::box gold | WAYS OUT
- File it, stamp it, and walk out past the porter with the paperwork showing.
- Run for the clock and take the count on the way, and hope four minutes is four minutes.
- Leave both boxes on the desk and let the Auditor decide which one is in order.
:::
:::

:::beats
1. **Alarm 3.** The gargoyles stop challenging and start following.
2. **Alarm 5.** The Auditor comes down the stair, unhurried, reading as it walks.
3. **Midnight, and the count.** Wren is Dean, and the Union stops being banned in the worst possible way.
:::

:::cols 2
:::stat Deanery Gargoyle | CR 2 | AC 15 | HP 52 | SPD 30 ft, fly 60 ft | STR +4 | DEX +2 | CON +4 | INT -2 | WIS +0 | CHA -2
**Two claws** +5, 5 ft: 7 (1d6+4) slashing each. It will not pursue past the landing.
**Challenge first.** It asks your business before doing anything at all. A correct answer stops it dead, and a filed form is a correct answer.
**Immune** poison, exhaustion. **Resists** non-magical damage while perched and still.
> Landing and stair · alarm 1: one · alarm 3: four · alarm 5: all of them
:::

:::stat The Auditor | CR 12 | AC 20 | HP 210 | SPD 30 ft | STR +5 | DEX +2 | CON +6 | INT +8 | WIS +7 | CHA +4
**Do not fight this.** It is on the page so you know exactly what coming down that stair looks like.
**Struck from the record** +11, 10 ft: 33 (6d10) force, and on a kill the target's enrolment is expunged.
**In order?** As an action it reads one document. If the document is in order it stamps it and leaves. That is the whole encounter, and it is winnable.
> Arrives at alarm 5 · one · it goes to the paperwork first, every time
:::
:::

:::cols 2
:::puzzle The Correct Form | the Vault · a heist won at a counter
LOOKS: A queue, a pen on a chain, and a rack of forms, one of which is a Substitution of Sealed Material.
SOLVE: Fill it in, stamp it with anything off the rack, and join the queue. The queue is the price, it takes real minutes, and midnight is a real deadline.
FAIL: Never a dead end. A wrong form still stamps -- it just files the swap under the wrong department, and somebody reads it in a fortnight.
:::

:::item The Eleventh Seal | Wondrous, rare -- the Vault, third shelf, unlabelled
Legend in a script nobody in Registry can read. In use, origin not established. It countersigns anything, and everything it countersigns is accepted by every office in the university, including the ones that do not exist.

Professor Emeritus Brand gives the annual public lecture in eleven days and last year six people came. Draconics needs something to put on the stand: a scale, fresh cut, not shed. Or an egg, viable, whole. They have already printed the posters.
!! THEME -- a heist where the mark is asleep, and the entire job is whether today is day eight or day nine.
:::

:::read ON THE BOARD -- AS POSTED
Professor Emeritus Halloway Brand gives the annual public lecture in eleven days. Attendance last year was six. Four of the six were asleep. One was Brand's wife. The department does not survive another six.

A scale, fresh cut, not shed. Or an egg, viable, whole. We have four hundred shed scales in a drawer and that is precisely the problem. A shed scale is a fingernail. A cut scale is a story.
:::

:::cols 2
:::box plain | ON THE WAY THERE
Five days north into the Teeth. The wyverns above the mine workings are not dragons and will kill you for the confusion.

The old one over the Golden Ore workings sleeps eight days in nine and nobody has established which day. A cut scale bleeds and is noticed. Eggs are counted -- reliably, obsessively, by every account. Draconics owns a cooler on a trailer and has already had the posters printed.
:::

2. The eggs are counted by the wyverns, not the dragon. Take one and they follow you home, all the way home.
3. Brand can tell a cut scale from a shed one at ten feet. Everyone in the department assumes he cannot.
4. The egg is viable and eleven days from hatching. The lecture is in eleven days.
5. The department already has the cooler, the stand and a thousand posters. The decision was made before the job was posted.

:::read WHERE IT STARTS -- READ IT FIVE DAYS IN, LOOKING UP
The mine crew below the roost have been counting the sleeping days for years, and they will tell you, confidently, that today is day eight. They are one out and they do not know it. Above you, warm air comes off the ledge in a slow steady column, and it is going up on a nine-count, not an eight.
:::

:::beats
1. **Five days in.** The crew give you the count, for free, over food, and they are certain, and they are wrong.
2. **The approach**, under the wyverns, who are not dragons and who will kill you for the confusion.
3. **The ledge.** The warm column, and a shoulder that is figure 41 made real at forty feet across.
4. **Nine seconds at the vessel.** Count them out loud at the table. Do not roll for them.
5. **The neighbouring scale moves.** The plate says it will. Somebody has to be holding it when it does.
6. **The nest shelf**, if they want six hundred rather than two-fifty -- and something has already counted it.
7. **Five days back**, in the weather, with eleven days to the lecture and a thousand posters already printed.
:::

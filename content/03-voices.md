One physical thing. One verbal thing. One want. That's a character.

:::npc Dean Fennimore Quill | Halfling · a thematic costume for every occasion
WANT: Get Eldoria back into the top ten of the Provincial Register.
VOICE: Fast, warm, closing a sale. Never stops walking -- do every conversation as a walk-and-talk.
BREAK: He cannot hold the shape of bad news. His brain converts it to an opportunity on arrival.
LINE: That's not a problem, that's a **narrative**.
:::

:::npc Bursar Hollis Grasp | Human · the real antagonist until Session 8
WANT: The budget to balance. That is genuinely, entirely it.
VOICE: Slow. Quiet. Two full seconds of silence before answering. Never blinks first.
BREAK: An unaccounted-for coin. Any amount. He will stop everything.
LINE: There is a form for that. You will not enjoy it.
:::

:::roll 1d6 | Who the party runs into in the corridor
1 A porter carrying a door. Just a door.
2 Two students arguing about a grade neither of them received.
3 Quill, mid-costume-change, delighted to see them.
4 Grasp, holding a ledger, saying nothing.
5 A cat that has been formally enrolled since 1387.
6 The party, coming the other way. It lasts one second.
:::

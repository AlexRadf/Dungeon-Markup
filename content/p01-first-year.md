:::pitch
Welcome to Eldoria. You are a first-year here, and so is the person you are about to play, and neither of you knows where anything is.
!! That is the correct amount to know.
:::

:::box dark | THE WHOLE GAME IS FOUR LINES
**One.** The GM says where you are and what is happening.

**Two.** You say what you do about it. In your own words.

**Three.** If it might not work, you roll a twenty-sided die.

**Four.** The GM says what happens next. Go back to One.
:::

:::box gold | THE SENTENCE THAT ALWAYS WORKS
**"I'd like to try to ______. Can I?"**

Fill in anything at all. Climb it, charm it, steal it, set fire to it, ask it a question.

You will never need to know a rule. You need to say what you want.
:::

There is no losing, no wrong move and no wasted turn. Nobody at this table knows all the rules — watch how often the person who has played longest looks something up.

+++

## Six numbers

Your sheet has six scores on it. Each one is a question. Bigger is better. That is the whole of it.

| | IT ASKS | SO USE IT FOR |
|---|---|---|
| **STR** | *Can I move it?* | Doors, grabbing, hitting, carrying your friend |
| **DEX** | *Can I do it neatly?* | Sneaking, balancing, locks, pockets, bows |
| **CON** | *Can I take it?* | Poison, cold, exhaustion, staying upright |
| **INT** | *Do I already know this?* | Books, magic, puzzles, working it out |
| **WIS** | *Do I notice?* | Spotting things, reading people, first aid |
| **CHA** | *Can I talk them round?* | Persuading, lying, threatening, performing |

Do not know which one to use? Say what you are doing and let the GM pick. That is their job.

## The die

:::cols 2
:::box plain | ROLL IT
Roll the **d20**. Add the small number next to what you are doing. Say the total out loud.

Higher is better. The GM has a target in mind and will tell you.
:::

:::box plain | TWO DICE INSTEAD OF ONE
**Advantage** — roll two, keep the better. Something is helping you.

**Disadvantage** — roll two, keep the worse. Something is against you.
:::
:::

**You will fail about a third of the time, and that is the good part.** A story where everything works is a story where nothing happened.

+++

## Your turn in a fight

A fight is the same game with an order to it. On your turn you get three things, in any order you like.

:::cols 2
:::box plain | MOVE
Walk, run, climb, back off, get behind something. About six squares, and you can split it up.
:::

:::box plain | DO ONE THING
Swing. Cast. Shove them in the pond. Pull the lever. Grab your friend. Anything you can describe.
:::
:::

**And say something, free of charge.** Shout a warning, make a threat, ask a question. Talking never costs you your turn.

:::box gold | AT ZERO HIT POINTS YOU ARE NOT DEAD
You fall over. Each turn you roll a d20 — **ten or more is good.** Any healing at all, of any amount, and you are back up.

Your friends will come and get you. That is what the game is about.
:::

## Nobody minds

:::valve
I will slow everyone down: For about three sessions. Nobody minds. They all did it.
I do not know the rules: Neither do they.
My idea is stupid: Say it anyway. Stupid ideas are the entire history of this hobby.
I am not good at voices: Then do not do voices. *He says he wants us to leave* is real play.
I will ruin it: The only way to do that is to stop taking part.
:::

+++

## When you have no idea what to do

:::beats
1. **"What do I see?"** Ask for the room again. Half the time the answer is in the second description.
2. **"What would my character know about this?"** The most useful sentence in the game. They have lived here and you have not.
3. **"What is my character afraid of?"** Play that. Fear makes better choices than tactics.
4. **"What would be the most fun to watch?"** Not the safest. Not the cleverest.
5. **"What do you lot think?"** Ask the others. Nobody is scoring you on your own.
:::

:::box dark | SIX THINGS YOU CAN SAY, WORD FOR WORD
**"I'd like to try to ______. What do I roll?"**

**"What would my character know about that?"**

**"Can I have a closer look at the ______?"**

**"Can I roll to talk them round?"**

**"I'm not sure what my options are. What could I do?"**

**"I'd rather we skipped that bit."** — always allowed, no reason needed.
:::

**Ask questions constantly.** A table where somebody asks a question every five minutes is a table that is going well.

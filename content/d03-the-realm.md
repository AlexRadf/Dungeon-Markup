:::cols 2
:::box dark | THIS SHEET IS YOURS
The players have the **Notice Board**. That is the whole world as far as they know it: seven jobs, all of them within an afternoon of the gate.

This is the sheet that says what is past the gate. Show it when somebody buys a map, draws one, or asks a carter — and show them a *worse* one than this. A map somebody sold you is wrong about at least one road.

**Every job on the board is on campus** except **Job 1**, which goes two days north to Snakebite Crag, in the Dragon's Teeth foothills off the Briarwood road. That is the party's first look at any of this.
:::

**A day is twenty-four miles of Kingsroad on foot**, thirty on a horse, and half of either off it. The Kingsroad is patrolled, drained and posted; everything else is a track that somebody's cart made. Off the road, add a day for the Mire, the high passes, or the deep Silverweald, and roll on the panel overleaf when they camp.

:::box red | THE THREE THINGS THIS MAP DOES NOT SAY
**Nothing on it is more than a week away.** That is the point of the Kingsroad and the reason the University can send undergraduates anywhere. Do not add a fortnight of travel to make somewhere feel far — make it feel far by what is standing in the road.

**No borders, no kingdoms, no war.** There is a King, because there is a Kingsroad and there are patrols on it, and that is all the politics this campaign has needed so far. Add the rest when a player asks a question that requires it.

**The Ruins of Astoria and the Great Mire are the two large empty spaces**, one drowned and one flooded. They are where a campaign goes after the notice board, and neither of them has an answer yet, which is deliberate — do not decide what is in Astoria until a player tells you what they are hoping is in Astoria.
:::

### The six places, and the three things you say about each

:::cols 2
:::place The Dragon's Teeth Mountains | North. Snow, ore, and older things than either
- The air is thin enough that everybody is quietly short-tempered
- Cart tracks in the pass, going up, none coming back down
- Somewhere above you, unhurried, something with a wingspan
:::

:::place The Silverweald Forest | East. Old growth, druid-kept, and it notices
- The light comes down green and the sound goes up and does not come back
- Every third tree is marked at shoulder height, in the same hand
- The Eldertree is visible from anywhere in the wood, which is not possible
:::
:::

:::cols 2
:::place The Great Mire | South-east. Wet, flat, and full of the tops of things
- Water to the knee for a mile, and then abruptly to the chest
- The Sunken Tower's top three floors, and lamplight in one of them
- Lizardfolk sign — cut reeds, laid deliberately, pointing away
:::

:::place The Merfolk Sea and the Shattered Isles | West. Trade, weather, and older treaties
- The Whispering Shores are named for the noise and the noise is not wind
- Highport's harbour bell rings for arrivals; it has rung twice at night this month
- The Isles are close enough to row to and nobody rows to them
:::
:::

:::cols 2
:::place Oakhaven | The town the University sits on top of, and pretends not to
- Every third building is student lodgings and every fourth is a pub
- The market runs on University coin and resents it
- The town watch and Campus Security do not speak, and both want you to know why
:::

:::place The Kingsroad | The realm's one reliable thing, and the reason anywhere is reachable
- Milestones every mile, King's arms on them, and half of them defaced
- A wayhouse every twelve miles: fire, floor space, no questions, two coppers
- Whoever you passed this morning, you will pass again this evening
:::
:::

:::cols 2
:::roll d10 | ON THE ROAD, OR IN CAMP
1 A carter with a broken axle who will pay in gossip and cheese.
2 Pilgrims for the Mystic Stonehenge, badly equipped, cheerful.
3 A King's patrol, four strong, wanting names and papers.
4 Somebody from the University, out here, who should not be.
5 Rain for a day and a half. Everything is wet and stays wet.
6 A wayhouse already occupied by people who stop talking.
7 Wolves — or the noise of wolves, which is not always wolves.
8 A milestone defaced in fresh paint, and the paint is a name.
9 A body off the verge, a week old, robbed of everything but boots.
10 A merchant train that will take passengers, for a favour later.
:::

:::box gold | HOW TO USE THIS WHEN THEY LEAVE CAMPUS
The jobs are a campus game and the map is not, so the join is where it gets interesting: **every place on this sheet already has somebody from the University in it.** A field trip, a dig, a survey, a graduate nobody has heard from, a lecturer's inconvenient brother.

That gives you a reason for the party to be sent, a person to meet when they arrive, and an argument to walk into. Pick the place, roll the department on **The Screen**, and you have the whole of next term.
:::

### Everything named on the map

:::rooms KIND | ON THE MAP | WHAT IT IS, AND WHAT IT IS FOR
Town | Highport | The realm's port. Customs, warehouses, a harbour bell and three shipping houses -- anything that arrives by sea, and anybody leaving quickly
Town | Stonehaven | Quarry town in the north-west, stone-built, suspicious of strangers -- the mason work on every building in Oakhaven came from here
Town | Briarwood | Timber and charcoal on the north-east road, one day out -- the last inn before the mountains, and where Job 1 stops for the night
Town | Riverbend | River port on the Eldrun, a day south. Barges, eels, smugglers -- freight the Kingsroad does not see
Town | Fisher's Creek | Small, east, at the Silver Stream. Fish, dye, and druid trade -- the way into the Silverweald that does not go past the Circle
Town | Frostpeak Hold | The last hold before the ice, six days north, walled and cold -- a destination, not a stop. Nobody goes to Frostpeak on the way
Landmark | The Ancient Eldertree | The heart of the Silverweald, and visible from all of it -- the one place in the wood where a promise is binding
Landmark | The Druid's Circle | Standing stones at the north edge of the forest, still attended -- permission. Nothing large happens in the Silverweald without them
Landmark | The Mystic Stonehenge | On the eastern plain, older than the Circle and not druid work -- astronomy, ley lines, and three University theses that disagree
Landmark | The Hidden Valley | A green cleft in the foothills, missed by every road -- somewhere to put a thing that must not be found
Landmark | Hunter's Rest | A lodge on the forest's east side. Beds, dogs, a hard-eyed keeper -- guides, and the only honest local information about the wood
Landmark | Lighthouse Rock | A lit tower on a stack off the Whispering Shores -- it is still lit. Somebody is still lighting it
Ruin | The Ruins of Astoria | A drowned city on the north-west shore, three days out -- the realm's big empty question, and the one worth a campaign
Ruin | The Crypt of Kharn | A barrow field south of Astoria, opened and re-sealed twice -- a short, nasty delve with a name attached to it
Ruin | The Sunken Tower | Three floors of a wizard's tower above the Mire, the rest below -- the Mire's landmark, and lit at night by somebody
Ruin | The Bog Village | Stilts and rotten walkways in the south-east Mire, mostly empty -- mostly
Dungeon | Wyvern's Lair | A high shelf in the Dragon's Teeth, fouled and loud -- what Job 1's kobolds are frightened of
Dungeon | Wyvern's Mines | Worked-out galleries under the lair, shored and abandoned -- a way up into the mountains that avoids the pass
Dungeon | Golden Ore Mines | Still working, on the north-east flank. Company guards, company scrip -- employment, and a strike that is going to turn ugly
Dungeon | The Griffon's Roost | Cliffs south-west of Oakhaven, and a nesting pair -- eggs. Everybody wants the eggs. Nobody has thought it through
Dungeon | The Bandit's Hideout | South-east, off the Silverweald road, badly hidden on purpose -- the obvious answer to a robbery, which is why it is not the answer
Dungeon | The Lizardman Encampment | Reed halls at the Mire's heart, moved every season -- not hostile. Not remotely interested in being anybody's dungeon
:::

:::box plain | TWO LABELS ON THIS MAP ARE WORN PAST READING
One by the mountains, one on the coast road south-west of Oakhaven. They are not in the list above, because nobody at this table can read them either.

Leave them. The first time a player squints at the map and asks what that says, the answer is whatever you need it to be that week — and it will be the only place on the sheet they actually remember.
:::

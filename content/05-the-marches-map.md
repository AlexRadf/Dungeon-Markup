### Reading the ground

| CHAR | GROUND | ON THE PAPER |
|---|---|---|
| `@` | The holding | Filled dot -- Kettlewick, and the only bed |
| `*` | A built thing | A house outline -- somebody raised it |
| `^` | Mountain | A peak, three watches to cross |
| `h` | Hills | Two low bumps |
| `f` | Forest | Three trees |
| `,` | Heath | Three tufts of grass |
| `=` | Marsh | Tufts standing in water |
| `~` | Open water | Two wave lines. Boat, or go round |
| `:` | Burnt waste | Fine hatching. Nothing grows |
| `.` | Open ground | Bare paper |

A coordinate is **column then row**, two digits each, printed inside the hex: 0403 is the fourth column, third row. It is the only index this book has -- a player points at the paper, reads four numbers, and those four numbers are the heading on the entry.

### The four rings

| RING | HEXES ON THIS MAP |
|---|---|
| One | 0101 0201 0102 0202 0203 0303 0104 0204 0105 0205 |
| Two | 0301 0401 0302 0402 0403 0304 0404 0305 0405 0503 |
| Three | 0501 0601 0502 0602 0603 0504 0604 0505 0605 0703 |
| Four | 0701 0702 0704 0705 |

The ring is how many hexes lie between it and Kettlewick, banded into four, and it is the whole difficulty dial: one is a first level's day out, four ends a ninth level party that arrives casual. Nothing out there levels up when the characters do.
:::

:::hex 0103 | Kettlewick | Holding -- ring 0 -- you are standing in it
SEE: One chimney line, a palisade nobody maintains, a gate that is shut at the second whistle whoever is still outside it.
HOOK: The Ledger, the tariff, the counter map with thirty-four hexes still blank, and a bursar who will pay for every one.
RISK: Nothing that will kill you. A debt, a rival party, a name written in the missing column with your name under it.
TAKE: Bed, board, a hireling who has been to ring one, forty pounds of blank survey stone, three rumours a night.
LEADS: Everywhere. 0102 and 0203 are one day out and back, which is why every first-year files one of them first.
:::

:::pitch
Residents of Dorm 4 have reported terrible late-night howling interrupting exam prep. Campus Security wants the source found, pacified **without being killed**, and walked to Alchemy for the cure. Thirty-five gold and a temporary clearance card.
:::

:::box dark | WHAT IS ACTUALLY HAPPENING -- READ THIS FIRST
**Leo Ashby**, second-year Zoology, was brewing himself a wolfsbane cure for a lycanthropy he caught on a field trip and never reported. He got the ratio wrong -- 5:1 instead of 3:1 -- and tonight is a full moon.

He is a werewolf, he is a weak one (35 hp), and **he is not hunting anybody.** He is in the Botanical Conservatory because it is the only dark quiet place on campus, and he is howling because every noise on this campus is currently arriving in his head at four times the volume. The howling is distress, not threat display.

**The two Campus Guards are the actual danger.** They have silvered batons, they are frightened, and their orders say *silvered force authorised*. If the party does nothing, the guards corner Leo in the propagation beds and put him down.

**It ends at Alchemy.** The finished cure is a ten-minute walk away and takes one dose. Leo has an unfinished flask in his coat pocket, which is not the cure but proves he was trying. Getting him there alive, and getting the guards to stand down, is the whole job.
:::

:::read STRONG START -- READ IT AT THE FOOT OF DORM FOUR
You are still crossing the quad when a clawed hand the size of a dinner plate comes through the second-floor stained glass. Coloured shards go everywhere. Then the howl -- and it goes on much too long, and it does not sound angry. Somewhere behind you a whistle blows, twice.
:::

:::cols 2
:::beats
1. **The RAs.** Three of them on the landing, all talking at once. They can tell you it is Leo, that he has been odd for a fortnight, and that he ran toward the greenhouse.
2. **Leo's room.** DC 10 Investigation: the ripped journal page, a wolfsbane still, and a fortnight of wrong ratios written out.
3. **The quad.** The guards are ahead of you and moving fast. Somebody has to get between them and the glasshouse.
4. **The conservatory.** Dark, hot, full of things that cut. Leo is at the far end with his back to the propagation beds.
5. **Pacify him.** Light, noise and shouting make it worse. Quiet, dark and a familiar voice make it better.
6. **The walk to Alchemy.** Ten minutes, across open campus, with a large wolf and two armed men who want to be told what to do.
:::

:::npc Leo Ashby | Zoology, second year · terrified, deafened, enormous
WANT: For everything to be quieter. That is the entire content of his mind right now.
VOICE: He cannot speak. He whines, flinches at sound, and covers his ears with both hands, which is worse than growling.
BREAK: He caught it on a field trip in March and never reported it, because reporting it means being sent down.
LINE: -- (he taps his coat pocket, twice, and looks at you)
USE: The flask in his pocket is the proof. Show it to the guards and they stop being guards and start being helpful.
:::
:::

:::secrets
1. Leo is not aggressive. He is overwhelmed -- every sound on campus is arriving four times too loud.
2. He hid an unfinished wolfsbane flask in his coat pocket. It is not a cure. It is evidence he was trying.
3. Silver breaks the transformation instantly *and* deals heavy damage. It is the fast answer and it may kill him.
4. Soothing sound works: DC 13 Performance, or any Arcana check that dampens noise, pacifies him for ten minutes.
5. The guards are the real threat. Left alone they corner him in the beds inside four rounds.
6. Alchemy has the finished cure on a shelf and will hand it over to anybody with the clearance card.
:::

:::item Campus Dining Hall VIP Pass | Wondrous, common -- from Security, with the fee
Free meals for a semester at any campus hall, no queue. Worth considerably more than thirty-five gold to anybody who has met the queue.
:::

**Light:** moonlight through glass, and every lamp anybody lights makes Leo worse. **The `:` beds** are razor-leaved specimens: 1d4 slashing to enter, 1d4 to leave, and Leo will not cross them.

:::rooms | AREA | WHAT IS THERE
1 | Leo's room | Broken window, a wolfsbane still, a fortnight of failed ratios, the journal page. DC 10 Investigation.
2 | The quad | Open ground, two guards crossing fast. Whoever reaches the greenhouse first decides how this ends.
3 | The conservatory door | Iron and glass, propped. Shutting it buys three rounds of quiet, and Leo notices at once.
4 | The upper walkway | Iron grating ten feet up, rings under boots. Anybody up here is a problem for him.
5 | The propagation beds | Where he goes to ground, razor beds on three sides. **The guards corner him here.**
:::

:::cols 2
:::stat Leo, Transformed | CR 2 | AC 12 | HP 35 | SPD 40 ft | STR +4 | DEX +2 | CON +3 | INT -1 | WIS +0 | CHA -2
**Claw** +5, 5 ft: 7 (1d6+4) slashing -- and **only** as a reaction to being cornered, grabbed or hit.
**Sensory overload.** Loud noise or bright light within 30 ft: he Dashes away from it, through whatever is in the way.
**Quiet.** Ten seconds of dark and near-silence, or a DC 13 Performance: he sits down and stays sat down.
**Immune** to non-silvered non-magical weapons. Silver ends the transformation at once and deals full damage.
> Area 5 · one · he has no attack that starts a fight and no interest in having one

**Silvered baton** +4, 5 ft: 6 (1d8+2) bludgeoning, dropping Leo out of wolf shape on any hit.
**Under orders.** They advance on the howling each round unless somebody with the clearance card says otherwise.
**They do not want to.** Any Persuasion success, or the flask, and they stand down for good.
> Area 2, then 5 · x2 · the timer on this session, not the monster
:::
:::

:::puzzle Making It Quiet | area 5 · ten seconds of nothing
LOOKS: A very large wolf with both hands over its ears, backed into the razor beds, flinching at the walkway overhead.
SOLVE: Kill the lights, get everybody off the grating, stop shouting. Ten seconds of dark and quiet is enough; a DC 13 Performance does it faster, and so does any spell that dampens sound. Then walk him out.
FAIL: Not a dead end. Noise, light, or a guard on the walkway and he Dashes -- through the beds, through the glass, out into the quad, and you start again somewhere worse.
:::

:::box gold | HOW IT ENDS
**They walk him to Alchemy.** One dose, ten minutes, done -- Leo is a mortified undergraduate by midnight. Full fee, the dining hall pass, and Zoology quietly owes the party a favour.

**Silver.** Fast and certain, and he takes 1d8+2 on the way back to human. He lives, furious and grateful at once, and Security writes it up as a success.

**The guards get there first.** Leo dies in the beds. The fee is paid, nobody at Dorm 4 speaks to the party again, and the journal page turns up at the inquest.

**He gets out into the quad.** A chase across a campus full of students at eleven at night, with the town Guard twenty minutes away.
:::

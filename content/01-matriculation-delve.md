:::pitch
You are not a student yet. Registration requires a stamp, the stamp is three floors underground, and the office that holds it closed eleven years ago.

**Theme** -- Nobody knows what they're doing. Nobody ever will. That's fine.
:::

## The beats

:::beats
1. **Roll call.** Each player states name and reason for enrolling, in character. Quill mispronounces every one.
2. **Rejected.** Grasp returns their paperwork. Enrolment is invalid without Form 7, stamped below by the holding officer.
3. **The descent.** One lantern, one stick of chalk, one Form 7 each. Nobody accompanies them.
4. **There is no officer.** The note on the desk says *back in ten minutes* and is dated eleven years ago.
5. **They stamp their own forms.** Grasp accepts them without a flicker and files them.
:::

:::read
Form 7 is four pages long. Three of them are the same page. The fourth asks for the name of your holding officer, and there is no line to write it on.
:::

:::clue THE CLUE -- S1
The die on the stamp reads **OFFICE OF SOMNOLENT MAINTENANCE**. Field Party 7 is now registered under a department decommissioned eleven years ago.
!! You are the Office of Somnolent Maintenance.
:::

:::track
Lantern oil: 6
Patience of the Bursar: 4
:::

:::valve
If it's dragging: The lantern runs low and the way back up is longer than the way down.
If it's going fast: Grasp finds a second missing signature and sends them straight back down.
:::

:::pitch
You are not a student yet. Registration requires a stamp, the stamp is three floors underground, and the office that holds it closed eleven years ago. Nobody is coming with you.
!! THEME -- nobody knows what they're doing. Nobody ever will. That's fine.
:::

:::read OPENING SHOT -- READ IT, THEN STOP TALKING
The Great Hall. Two hundred first-years. Dean Quill, in a gold-stitched phoenix gown, arms wide: "Welcome -- to THE ELDORIA EXPERIENCE." Behind him, a man with a ledger has not looked up once.
:::

:::cols 2
## The beats

:::beats
1. **Roll call.** Each player says name and reason for enrolling, in character. Quill mispronounces every one. They are now, as far as this room believes, students.
2. **Rejected.** Grasp returns the paperwork. Enrolment is invalid without **Form 7**, stamped below by the holding officer. Every first-year does this. It has been a formality for three hundred years.
3. **The descent.** One lantern, one stick of chalk, one Form 7 each. Nobody accompanies them, and nobody has for some time.
4. **Three rooms.** Animate homework. A flooded run with a very polite ooze. The Deep Stack itself, unsorted and enormous.
5. **There is no officer.** The office is empty. The note on the desk says *back in ten minutes*, dated eleven years ago.
6. **The sealed door.** A painted-over nameplate. Behind it: four desks, a wall map, and a stamp that still has ink in it.
7. **They stamp their own forms.** Grasp accepts them without a flicker and files them. He knows exactly what he is looking at.
:::

## The cast tonight

WANT: Eldoria back in the Provincial Register's top ten.
VOICE: Fast, warm, closing a sale. Never stops walking.
BREAK: He cannot hold bad news. It converts to an opportunity on arrival.
LINE: That's not a problem, that's a **narrative**.

:::npc Bursar Hollis Grasp | Human · Finance Office · the real antagonist until S8
WANT: The budget to balance. That is genuinely, entirely it.
VOICE: Slow, quiet. Two full seconds of silence before answering.
BREAK: An unaccounted coin. Any amount. He stops everything.
LINE: There is a form for that. You will not enjoy it.
:::

:::clue THE CLUE -- S1 -- THE LAST BEAT OF THE NIGHT
The die on the stamp reads **OFFICE OF SOMNOLENT MAINTENANCE**. Field Party 7 is now registered under a department decommissioned eleven years ago, and is on paper its only staff.
!! You are the Office of Somnolent Maintenance.
>> Do not tell them. It is why they can file a new lease in Session 10.
:::

:::cols 2
### What you'll actually roll
Keep every DC at or under 13 -- this is the tutorial. Navigate: Survival or Investigation DC 12. The ooze: Persuasion DC 13. One short rest, in the dark, at their own suggestion.

### Play with
- The welcome punch is **cursed**. Anyone who drinks it rhymes for an hour. Quill has four cups and gives a *better* speech.
- A Deep Stack book is overdue since **1189**. The fine is quoted in gold, then in decades of service.
- **Plant the oath:** a proctor complains that they can't help anyone during a practical. Nobody cares. Yet.
:::

:::valve
If it's dragging: The lantern runs low and the way back up is longer than the way down.
If it's going fast: Grasp finds a second missing signature and sends them straight back down.
Cliffhanger: That night a docket is pushed under the door of Tower Room 0 -- eleven years undelivered, released the moment the seal was used again. Hand it over and stop there.
:::

The delve every first-year does to get Form 7 stamped. Registration is the hook; the sealed office -- and what it makes them -- is the point. **Light:** lantern only. Beyond 30 ft is rumour.

:::rooms | AREA | WHAT IS THERE
1 | The registry stair | Down from the queue. The door sticks shut -- DC 12 Athletics, or press on. A steam pipe vents nearby.
2 | Collapsed reading room | A fallen shelf, eighty years of essays. **Homework Swarm x2** rises when anyone reads aloud. Under the shelf: a *Marginal Note*.
3 | The flooded run | Knee-deep and moving. **Gutterwick** lives in the pipe. Difficult terrain, DC 12 Acrobatics or prone.
4 | The holding office | A cold cup and a note: *back in ten minutes*, dated eleven years ago. Third-years squat here, with a stolen faculty key good for one door.
5 | The sealed office | **THE CLUE.** Four desks. In the drawer, the Somnolent Stamp, ink still wet. Secret door DC 14 -- or just push. Not locked, only forgotten.
:::

### What is down there -- everything you need is on this page

:::puzzle The Painted-Over Nameplate | Area 5 · two minutes, not twenty
LOOKS: No handle, and a rectangle of thicker paint at eye height.
SOLVE: Anything that reads or reveals -- a wet rag, light at an angle. It says OFFICE OF SOMNOLENT MAINTENANCE. Saying it aloud is the key: the door was sealed by record, not lock.
FAIL: Never a dead end. If they stall, the third-years in area 4 sell them "the room nobody's assigned to" for one ration.
:::

:::cols 3
### Hazards
**Steam vent** *(area 1)* Every 10 min: DC 11 DEX or 1d6 fire. Audible three rounds first.

**Deep water** *(area 3)* Difficult terrain.

### Roll d6
:::roll d6 | COMPLICATION
1 A pipe bursts. DC 12 DEX or soaked.
2 Singing. A third-year, lost.
3 The lantern gutters.
4 Loose scree. DC 11 or prone.
5 A rat with a message. Not theirs.
6 A rival party, already stamped.
:::

### Worth taking
:::item The Somnolent Stamp | Wondrous, rare -- area 5
Anything it stamps is filed and accepted as the act of a standing department.
:::

**The Marginal Note** *(area 2)* Once a day, ask it about a document in your hand. One snide sentence, always true.

**Budget.** 4 encounters, ~300 XP. Level 2 at the end regardless.
:::

:::pitch
Practical Zoology needs live specimens for next week's lecture: a clutch of intact kobold eggs, or a handful of fresh scales. There are active nests up Snakebite Crag, two days north. Fifty gold, and they would prefer everybody came back.
:::

:::box dark | WHAT IS ACTUALLY HAPPENING -- READ THIS FIRST
The kobolds are **not native to the crag.** Estates evicted them from the university boiler room last month and they walked here, carrying their eggs, and they are furious about it. They know exactly which building the party came from.

They revere dragon scales, so **they go for the heaviest armour first** -- not the biggest threat, the shiniest one. A plate-wearing fighter is a walking harvest and will be swarmed while the wizard is ignored.

The nursery sits over an **unstable geothermal vent.** Any fire, blast or hard impact near it sends up a gout: 2d6 fire in a 10 ft line, and one egg cracks. This is why nobody has cleared the nest already.

**Fresh scales are lying on the ground.** The nursery litter is full of shed and struck-off scales, and Zoology asked for eggs *or* scales. A party that reads the notice properly can fill the brief without touching an egg -- and one that talks to the Inventor can be handed a bagful, because the kobolds want a letter to Estates far more than they want a fight.
:::

:::read STRONG START -- READ IT ON THE ROAD NORTH
Something small and leathery comes out of the sky at head height, misjudges everything, and crashes flat onto the open map in front of you. It is a bat with a painted wooden tag tied round its neck. The tag reads, in careful pictures: a tower, a boot, and a great many kobolds, and then the same tower on fire.
:::

:::cols 2
:::beats
1. **Two days north**, and the napkin map is wrong about the bridge but right about everything else.
2. **The switchbacks.** The trail is watched from the moment they start climbing. Nothing attacks yet.
3. **The rockslide.** A propped bluff of scree rigged to a trip-line. DC 13 Perception to spot, DC 12 Dexterity with tools to disarm, or just go round and lose an hour.
4. **The bridge.** A rope crossing, one at a time, with Dragonshields waiting at the far end and slings above.
5. **The nursery.** Baskets over a hot vent, scales all over the floor, and the Inventor between the party and the eggs.
6. **Deal or raid.** Take scales off the floor and go, trade for a clutch, or fight for them over an unstable vent.
:::

:::npc Skit, the Kobold Inventor | Snakebite Crag · evicted, aggrieved, inventive
WANT: A letter from the university saying the boiler room was theirs. He cannot read it and will still want it.
VOICE: Fast, high, indignant. Counts grievances on his fingers and runs out of fingers.
BREAK: He built every trap on this crag in three weeks and is desperate for somebody to notice.
LINE: We *lived* there! Forty years! There was a *sign!*
USE: Compliment the traps and he will talk. Promise the letter and he will fill a bag with scales himself.
:::
:::

:::secrets
1. The kobolds were evicted from the university boiler room last month. They know where the party is from.
2. They target the heaviest armour first, to harvest the metal. It is reverence, not tactics, and it is exploitable.
3. The vent under the nursery erupts if anything strikes it: 2d6 fire in a 10 ft line, and an egg cracks.
4. Shed scales are all over the nursery floor. The job can be finished without taking a single egg.
5. One egg glows blue and hums. It is the wyrmling's, and the wyrmling knows exactly where it is at all times.
6. Skit will trade a whole bag of scales for a signed letter about the boiler room. Nobody has offered him anything.
:::

:::item Scroll of Acid Arrow | Scroll, uncommon -- Skit's workshop
Kobold-written, in pictures, and it works. Skit has been saving it for an emergency and has not recognised one yet.
:::

**Light:** daylight, and a crosswind that whistles. **The chasm** either side of area 3 is a sixty-foot drop onto jagged rock.

:::rooms | AREA | WHAT IS THERE
1 | The switchbacks | The only way up. Watched from the first step. Sling stones start if anybody draws a weapon here.
2 | The rockslide bluff | Propped scree on a trip-line. DC 13 Perception, DC 12 Dex to disarm. Triggered: DC 13 Dex or 2d6 bludgeoning, prone, at a cliff edge.
3 | The rope bridge | One square wide, sixty feet up, crosswind. **2x Dragonshields** hold the far end, two more sling from above. Fighting on it is a bad idea and they know that.
4 | The guard shelf | Skit's workshop: skunk-pots, hornet-cages, a half-built third thing. He is here, not at the nest.
5 | The vent nursery | Steaming, sulphurous, glowing clutch-baskets over a live vent. **Scales all over the floor.** One basket hums and glows blue.
:::

**It wants the blue egg.** It will not leave the nursery and will not pursue anybody who is not carrying that basket.
> Area 5 · one · put the basket down and the fight is over
:::

:::puzzle The Vent Nursery | area 5 · getting eggs out without setting them off
LOOKS: Eight woven baskets on a rock shelf, warm from below, over a fissure venting steam and the occasional spit of flame.
SOLVE: Move the baskets one at a time and by hand -- DC 12 Dexterity each, no rolling needed if nobody is being attacked. A wet cloak or a shield laid over the fissure suppresses it entirely for a minute. **Or take scales off the floor and leave the eggs where they are**, which is the whole job and costs nothing.
FAIL: The vent goes up: 2d6 fire in a 10 ft line, one egg cracks, and every kobold on the crag comes running.
:::

:::box gold | HOW IT ENDS
**Scales off the floor.** Nobody fights, nothing is stolen, the brief is met. Zoology pays the fifty gold and complains about presentation.

**Skit's bargain.** A written promise about the boiler room buys a full bag of scales and safe passage both ways. The letter is worthless. He will frame it.

**They take the eggs.** The clutch is worth the fee and the crag is now hostile ground -- Skit's traps are rebuilt within a week and the next party up finds six of them.

**They take the blue one.** The wyrmling follows them home. It arrives at the university two nights later, and that is somebody's next job.
:::

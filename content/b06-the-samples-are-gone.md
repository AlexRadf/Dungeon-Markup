:::pitch
Forty numbered geodes went into the polisher room on Tuesday. The polisher room now has a draught coming out of the back wall and no geodes in it, and a second-year teaching assistant has to grade them by eight in the morning out of her own pocket.
!! THEME -- the smallest fee on the whole board, and behind the wall is a baby the size of a pony.
:::

:::read WHERE IT STARTS -- READ IT WITH THE POLISHER STILL RUNNING
The polisher room is hot, and it is hotter at the back wall than it is at the boiler, which is the wrong way round entirely. Behind that wall is a steam tunnel on no plan of this building. The grate has been pushed out from the inside, and there is quartz grit on the floor in a trail leading in.
:::

Forty feet of tunnel, then it stops being a tunnel. **Light:** yours, and area 5 gives every bit of it straight back. **Heat:** DC 12 CON an hour below the grate, or a level of exhaustion.

:::rooms | AREA | WHAT IS THERE
1 | The polisher room | Hot, loud, thirty-nine geodes short. The grate was pushed **out**, from the tunnel side.
2 | The tunnel | Low, hot, forty feet. Tool marks start halfway and they are recent and neat. Lamp oil burning further in than Estates has ever been.
3 | The cut face | Worked obsidian, lamps lit, tools down mid-shift. **Crystal-clad miners x4**, carrying geodes one at a time, one way, and not towards the face.
4 | The sorting gallery | Thirty-nine numbered geodes, stacked beautifully. **Number 31 is open and empty.** The collection permit is here with Vance's name on it.
5 | The Tyrant's floor | Polished black, no cover, and it knows that. The beam wants a straight line and a shine -- break either and it has to come to you on foot.
:::

:::secrets
1. The geodes are eggs. Thirty-nine of them, in a crate, in a university building, one floor above a lecture theatre.
2. Number 31 is already open. That is what pushed the grate out.
3. The miners are the last field trip. Crack the crystal off one and you get a very cold, very cross second-year who wants to know what day it is.
4. The Tyrant is young -- pony-sized, brand new -- and quicker than anything these characters have met.
5. Its beam needs a straight line on polished stone. Break either and it is just a fast animal with a temper.
6. Vance signed nothing. The permit has her name on it and she has never seen it before in her life.
:::

:::cols 2
:::box red | TROUBLE
- Crystal-clad miners who neither stop nor hurry, and who were second-years in March.
- Refracted beams. On black glass there is no cover unless somebody makes some, loudly.
- The heat: an hour down here costs a CON save or a level of exhaustion.
- Every short rest is half an hour off a clock that ends at eight in the morning.
:::

:::box gold | WAYS OUT
- Kill it, which is the honest answer and the reason this is a dungeon and not an errand.
- Put the eggs back in the gallery and it settles, and nobody grades anything, and Vance is out fifteen gold.
- Drop the polishing wheel through the gallery floor and lose the grades along with the floor.
:::
:::

:::beats
1. **Eight o'clock**, and Vance is asked where the samples are, in front of people.
2. **The permit is produced.** It has her signature on it and she did not write it.
3. **Geology sends a second party at noon**, properly equipped, in numbers, with no idea at all.
4. **Thirty-nine eggs go up in a crate** to a warm departmental office, on a Friday, before a long weekend.
:::

:::cols 2
:::stat The Tyrant | CR 4 | AC 15 | HP 76 | SPD 40 ft, climb 30 ft | STR +2 | DEX +5 | CON +3 | INT +0 | WIS +2 | CHA +1
**Bite** +7, 5 ft: 11 (2d6+4) piercing, twice a turn, and it does not stay to trade blows.
**Refracted beam** (recharge 5–6). A straight line along polished stone, 60 ft: DC 14 DEX, 22 (5d8) radiant, half on a save. Bounces once off any polished surface, which is all of them.
**Needs the line.** Rubble, smoke, a dropped cloak, a cracked floor -- break the line or dull the stone and it cannot fire at all.
> The gallery floor · one · opens at range and closes only when it has to
:::

:::stat Crystal-Clad Miner | CR 1/2 | AC 14 | HP 30 | SPD 25 ft | STR +3 | DEX -1 | CON +3 | INT -3 | WIS +0 | CHA -4
**Pick** +5, 5 ft: 7 (1d8+3) piercing. It swings on the beat and never once off it.
**On the shift.** It will not touch anybody carrying a geode toward the gallery. Pick one up and walk along with them.
**Clad.** Resists slashing and piercing. Under the crystal is a second-year in a field jacket, and one of them still has a name tape.
> The cut face and the tunnel · x4 · breakable, and they say thank you
:::

:::cols 2
:::puzzle Thirty-One | the gallery · already open, and the lid is on the floor
LOOKS: Thirty-nine stacked, and one on its side with the top hinged back like a lid, and nothing inside it.
SOLVE: Count them. Everything they need tonight is arithmetic: thirty-nine here, forty on the sheet, nine lost to a collapse that never happened.
FAIL: They take the crate up without counting, and the session ends in a warm office at eight in the morning.
:::

:::pitch
Forty numbered geodes went into the polisher room on Tuesday. The polisher room now has a draught coming out of the back wall and no geodes in it, and a second-year teaching assistant has to grade them by eight in the morning out of her own money.
!! THEME -- the smallest fee on the board, for the worst thing behind any of the doors.
:::

:::read ON THE BOARD -- AS POSTED
Forty geodes, numbered, cut and collected by first years on the Stonehaven field trip. They went into the polisher room on Tuesday. The polisher room now has a draught coming out of the back wall and no geodes in it.

I have to grade them by eight. Bring back the numbered ones. I don't care about the rest. This is my own money, not the department's.
:::

:::cols 2
:::box plain | ON THE WAY THERE
The polisher room is in the basement of the Geology building and the basement connects to the steam tunnels, which everyone knows and nobody has mapped.

Vance is a second-year TA covering a module she did not design. The field trip lost nine samples to a tunnel collapse and the trip report says so. Estates say the back wall is solid. Estates said that about the pool. Nobody has seen the first-year who signed for the crate.
:::

:::box dark | HANDOUT -- THE GRADING SHEET, LEFT OPEN ON THE POLISHER BENCH
SPEC. 1–40, STONEHAVEN CUT. **12** -- banded, poor. **19** -- hollow, good crystal. **31** -- hollow, contents not mineral, **DO NOT CUT UNTIL VANCE PRESENT**. **32–40** -- not recovered, tunnel collapse, see trip report.
:::
:::

:::box plain | HANDOUT -- PEN & INK -- MINERALOGY PLATE
An engraved mineralogy plate. Four geodes in cross-section, ruled and numbered, interiors hatched, scale bar along the bottom. Three are ordinary -- radiating crystal, clean parallel lines, the flat competence of a teaching illustration. Specimen 31 is drawn with identical care, and the crystals inside it are curled, and they follow a shape, and the shape is foetal. Nothing exaggerated. The horror is that the engraver clearly did not think it worth remarking on. Caption: *Spec. 31. Sectioned from the drawing only. Not opened.*
:::

:::secrets
1. The geodes are eggs. Thirty-nine of them, in a crate, in a university building, upstairs from a lecture theatre.
2. Number 31 is already open. That is what pushed the grate out from the inside.
3. The miners are the previous field trip. The report says tunnel collapse because somebody had to write something.
4. The Tyrant is young -- pony-sized -- and faster than anything these characters have ever met.
5. Its beam needs a straight line and polished stone. Break either and it has to close the distance on foot.
6. The tunnel behind the back wall appears on no plan of this building, and the grate was pushed, not cut.
7. Vance signed nothing. The collection permit has her name on it and she has never seen it before.
8. Every short rest down here is half an hour off a clock that ends at eight in the morning.
:::

Forty feet of tunnel, then it stops being a tunnel. **Light:** yours only, and area 5 gives every bit of it straight back. **Heat:** DC 12 CON each hour below the grate or a level of exhaustion.

:::rooms | AREA | WHAT IS THERE
1 | The polisher room | Hot, loud, thirty-nine geodes short. The grate in the back wall was pushed **out**, from the tunnel side. Quartz grit in a trail leading in.
2 | The tunnel | Hot, low, forty feet. Tool marks start halfway along and they are recent and neat. Lamp oil burning further in than Estates has ever been.
3 | The cut face | Worked obsidian, lamps lit, tools down mid-shift. **Crystal-clad miners x4**, carrying geodes one at a time, one way, and not to the face.
4 | The sorting gallery | Thirty-nine numbered geodes stacked neatly. **Number 31 is open and empty.** The collection permit is here with Vance's name on it.
5 | The Tyrant's floor | Polished black, no cover, and it likes that. The beam needs a straight line and a shine — break either and it has to come to you.
:::

:::roll d6 | COMPLICATION -- ONCE AN HOUR, OR WHEN THEY REST
1 A lamp goes out. The floor still shines.
2 Steam vents. DC 12 DEX or 1d6 fire.
3 A miner says a name. It is a name.
4 A geode in someone's pack shifts.
5 The grate behind you closes.
6 Vance came down after you. Of course she did.
:::

:::track
Hours until eight in the morning: 8
:::

:::valve
If it's dragging: a geode in somebody's pack gets warm and then gets quiet.
If it's going fast: a second field trip's worth of tools, further in, older, and nobody attached to them.
Cliffhanger: eight o'clock, the crate goes up to the department, and thirty-eight is the count.
:::

:::cols 2
:::box red | TROUBLE
- Crystal-clad miners who neither stop nor hurry, and who were second-years in March.
- Refracted beams. On black glass there is no cover unless somebody makes some, loudly.
- The heat. Every hour down here costs a con save or a level of exhaustion at the GM's discretion.
- Every short rest is half an hour off the clock, and the clock ends at eight.
:::

:::box gold | WAYS OUT
- Kill it, which is the honest answer and the reason this is a dungeon and not an errand.
- Put the eggs back in the gallery and it settles, and nobody grades anything.
- Drop the polishing wheel through the gallery floor and lose the grades along with the floor.
:::
:::

:::beats
1. **Eight o'clock.** Vance is asked where the samples are.
2. **The collection permit is produced.** It has her signature on it and she did not write it.
3. **Geology sends a second party at noon**, properly equipped, in numbers, with no idea.
4. **Thirty-nine eggs go up to the department in a crate**, into a warm room, on a Friday.
:::

:::cols 2
:::stat The Tyrant | CR 4 | AC 15 | HP 76 | SPD 40 ft, climb 30 ft | STR +2 | DEX +5 | CON +3 | INT +0 | WIS +2 | CHA +1
**Bite** +7, 5 ft: 11 (2d6+4) piercing, twice per turn, and it does not stay to trade.
**Refracted beam** (recharge 5–6). One straight line along polished stone, 60 ft: DC 14 DEX, 22 (5d8) radiant, half on a save. It bounces once off any polished surface, which is every surface here.
**Needs the line.** Rubble, smoke, a dropped cloak, a cracked floor -- break the line or dull the stone and the beam cannot fire at all.
**Young.** Pony-sized, brand new, and faster than anything at this tier has any business being.
> The gallery floor · one · it opens at range and closes only when it has to
:::

:::stat Crystal-Clad Miner | CR 1/2 | AC 14 | HP 30 | SPD 25 ft | STR +3 | DEX -1 | CON +3 | INT -3 | WIS +0 | CHA -4
**Pick** +5, 5 ft: 7 (1d8+3) piercing. It swings on the beat and never off it.
**On the shift.** It will not attack anyone carrying a geode towards the gallery. Carry one and walk with them.
**Clad.** Resistant to slashing and piercing. Under the crystal is a second-year in a field jacket.
> The cut face, the tunnel · x4 · one of them still has a name tape
:::
:::

:::puzzle Thirty-One | the gallery · already open, and the lid is on the floor
LOOKS: Thirty-nine stacked, and one on its side with the top hinged back like a lid, and nothing inside.
SOLVE: Count them. Everything they need to understand tonight is arithmetic: thirty-nine here, forty on the sheet, nine lost to a collapse that did not happen.
FAIL: They take the crate up without counting, and the session ends in a warm departmental office at eight in the morning.
:::

:::item Specimen 19 | Mineral, uncommon, hollow -- the sorting gallery
Good crystal, banded, entirely ordinary, and the only one on the sheet that is worth anything. Sold honestly it covers Vance's fifteen gold three times over. It is also, on the sheet, immediately above the one that opened.
:::

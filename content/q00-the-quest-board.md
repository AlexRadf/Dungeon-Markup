**Cut on the solid line, fold on the dashed one, printed side out** -- fold it the other way and both halves end up inside, which is a lesson everybody learns once. Front: what the job is and what it pays. Back: the notice as it hangs, what they hear on the way, and the handouts worth making.

:::quest Something Is Eating Our Survey | JOB 1 · THE BOARD
GENRE: combat
THEME: monster hunt, tracking, feeding it
WHERE: The Iron Henge, two days north
POSTED: Metallurgy
PAYS: 40 gp
LEVEL: 1–2
DIFFICULTY: Easy
Three survey teams went up to the Iron Henge and one tripod came back with the brass licked off it. Metallurgy has written off the teams, the theodolite and quietly the whole hillside.

Find out what is taking the equipment. Bring the theodolite back if there is any of it left.
>> ON THE BOARD -- AS POSTED
Three teams sent to the Iron Henge. One tripod came back with the brass stripped off it. The instruments were department property.

Find what is taking our equipment and stop it taking any more. We are not asking anybody to be brave. Bring back the theodolite if there is anything left of it.
>> ON THE WAY THERE
The pillars are not iron, whatever everyone calls them. Eleven of them, black, taller than a person -- though farmers who use the pass swear there are nine. Compasses spin within fifty yards and sheep will not graze there. Cartography says the henge moves, but Cartography says that about everything.
>> HANDOUTS TO MAKE
- **An equipment loss form**, returned by Finance, Section 4 (Casualties) blank and the claim held pending it.
- **A specimen sheet** of seven numbered pellets of digested metal, one with a signet ring fused into it.

:::quest The Light Is Still On | JOB 4 · THE BOARD
GENRE: investigation
THEME: tide clock, mimicry, teaching
WHERE: Lighthouse Rock, off Highport
POSTED: Navigation & Maritime Law
PAYS: 75 gp
LEVEL: 3–5
DIFFICULTY: Medium
The keeper has filed nothing for eleven weeks and the light has burned every night of them, exactly on pattern. If the answer to *who has been lighting it* is nobody, the wrecks belong to the university.

Establish what has been lit, and by whom. Bring back the log.
>> ON THE BOARD -- AS POSTED
Lighthouse Rock has filed no keeper's log in eleven weeks. The light runs nightly, on pattern, which is our difficulty.

If the light was showing, the wrecks are the pilots' liability. If it wasn't, they are ours, and we cannot afford ourselves. The finding matters more than the rescue. We'd rather it didn't.
>> ON THE WAY THERE
Two days west to Highport, an hour of open water, and a rock that goes under at high tide. Maerin Vosk has kept the light nine years. Two ships have gone down on the reef since -- Highport says three -- and the pilots steer by it regardless. Hydrological Sorcery ran tide work off that coast in March and denies all of it.
>> HANDOUTS TO MAKE
- **Three pages torn out of the keeper's log**, ending with an entry dated three weeks from today.
- **A pilots' light-list page**: the printed flash pattern, and three hand-ruled observations that match neither the print nor each other.

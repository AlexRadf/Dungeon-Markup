**Cut on the solid line, fold on the dashed one, printed side out** -- fold it the other way and both halves end up inside, which is a lesson everybody learns once. **Front:** the notice as it hangs, and what the job is, pays and costs. **Back:** one handout. Paste a picture into the frame and it fills it; until you do, the frame prints the brief for drawing one. The guidance along the foot says what it is for.

:::quest Something Is Eating Our Survey | JOB 1 · THE BOARD
GENRE: combat
THEME: monster hunt, tracking, feeding it
WHERE: The Iron Henge, two days north
POSTED: Metallurgy
PAYS: 40 gp
LEVEL: 1–2
DIFFICULTY: Easy
Three teams sent to the Iron Henge. One tripod came back with the brass stripped off it. The instruments were department property.

Find what is taking our equipment. Stop it taking any more. We are not asking anybody to be brave. Bring back the theodolite if there is anything left of it.
>> PEN & INK -- GASTROLITHS RECOVERED, LOTS I–VII
ART: An engraved natural-history plate. Seven pellets in two rows, numbered, italic captions, smooth and faintly concentric like river stones -- stipple the surface, hatch the shadows. They are lumps of digested metal and the eye should take a second to get there. Lot III has a signet ring fused into it. Lot VII sits beside a six-inch scale bar and is much wider than the bar.
!! Hand it over before they set out. It is the only warning of scale they get, and somebody would like that ring back.
:::

:::quest The Cellar Has Grown | JOB 12 · THE BOARD
GENRE: dungeon
THEME: counting, wine, an impostor builder
WHERE: Under the dining hall
POSTED: The Bursar's office
PAYS: 70 gp and one bottle
LEVEL: 2–4
DIFFICULTY: Medium
The wine cellar has eleven bays. It has had eleven bays since the university opened. Tuesday's stocktake recorded nineteen. Friday's recorded twenty-six.

The wine in the new bays is very good. The wine in the new bays is not ours. The Centennial Dinner is in six days and this office will not serve the Chancellor a bottle it can't account for.
>> PEN & INK -- A LABEL OFF ONE OF THE NEW BOTTLES
ART: A wine label at actual size. Oval, engraved border, confident line work -- vine scroll, a small woodcut of a house on a hill, entirely convincing. !! Let somebody peel it off a bottle rather than handing it over. The university is in its ninety-ninth year and nobody needs telling that out loud.

:::quest The Dragon Lecture | JOB 14 · THE BOARD
GENRE: heist
THEME: climbing, nine seconds, no fighting
WHERE: The Dragon's Teeth, five days north
POSTED: Draconics
PAYS: 250 gp scale, 600 gp egg
LEVEL: 4–5
DIFFICULTY: Hard
Prof. Emeritus Halloway Brand gives the annual public lecture in eleven days. Attendance last year was six. Four were asleep. One was Brand's wife.

A scale, fresh cut, not shed. Or an egg, viable, whole. We have four hundred shed scales in a drawer and that is the problem. A shed scale is a fingernail. A cut scale is a story.
>> PEN & INK -- FIGURE 41, FROM BRAND'S OWN MONOGRAPH
ART: A naturalist's plate: cross-section through the shoulder of a dragon, layered scale in dense hatching, leader lines to lettered notes. **a.** the free edge -- lift here, and only here. **b.** the seated root -- two inches; deeper is bone, shallower is nothing. **c.** the vessel -- *avoid absolutely*; nine seconds of pressure and the subject is awake. **d.** the neighbouring scale -- it will move; do not let it move. Plate number, running head, folio: the kind of drawing that makes you briefly forget what it is a drawing of.
!! This is the puzzle. Give it to them five days out and let them plan off it, then count the nine seconds aloud at the ledge.
:::

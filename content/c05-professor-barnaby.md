:::pitch
Professor Barnaby turned himself into a cat at eight this morning and was immediately collected by Campus Pest Control. Alchemy will pay five hundred gold to have him back in his own lab before five, which is when their counter-agent goes off.
:::

:::box dark | WHAT IS ACTUALLY HAPPENING -- READ THIS FIRST
**"Campus Pest Control" is a front** for a goblin crime syndicate running an exotic-creature auction out of Boiler Room B. They have no idea the orange tabby in cage fourteen is a professor. They think they have a fire-breathing cat, which is worth considerably more.

**Barnaby is entirely himself.** He cannot speak or hold a pen, but he can read, he can count, and he will spell words out with a paw in the coal dust if anybody thinks to put some in front of him. He is furious, and he is far more worried about his syllabus deadline than about being a cat.

**The sneeze is the weapon.** He is violently allergic to dragon-pepper, and a sneeze produces a two-foot gout of flame. The syndicate learned this the hard way and keeps the spice crates at the far end of the strong room. A handful of dragon-pepper anywhere near cage fourteen is a distraction, a lockpick and a fire, all at once.

**Five o'clock is real.** Alchemy's counter-agent is brewed and sitting on a bench; after five it is inert and they need another week. Barnaby stays a cat, misses his own deadline, and takes it extremely personally. **The auction opens at 4:30** -- half an hour before the deadline, on purpose.
:::

:::read STRONG START -- READ IT IN THE QUAD, AT NINE IN THE MORNING
A teaching assistant catches you at a dead run, thrusts a paper bag into your hands and says three sentences without breathing: Professor Barnaby is a cat, Pest Control took him at half past eight, and the bag is Catnip of Detection and it points at him. The bag is already straining sideways, toward the Student Centre.
:::

:::beats
1. **The service stair.** Two goblins on the door, bored, pretending to be council staff. Bribe, bluff, or the catwalk overhead.
2. **The ducts.** Hissing pipes, red light, slick catwalks. Quiet, and hot, and the only way past the door guards.
3. **The boiler floor.** Cages, paperwork, the auction slip on a clipboard, and the strong room key on a hook.
4. **The strong room.** Dragon-pepper crates at the far end and, in cage fourteen, one extremely angry orange cat.
5. **Half past four: the auction.** The pit fills up. The Boss brings out Lot 14. A drow noble is bidding and is not the problem.
6. **The run home.** Across campus with a cat that does not want to be carried, before the clock hits five.
:::

:::npc Professor Barnaby | Alchemy · a cat · incandescent
WANT: To be a person again, and, more urgently, for the syllabus to go to the printer by five.
VOICE: None. He communicates by staring, by knocking things off shelves deliberately, and by paw-writing in dust.
BREAK: He did this to himself and knows exactly which reagent he mismeasured. He will not be discussing it.
LINE: -- (he writes **PEPPER** in the coal dust, underlines it twice, and looks at you)
USE: Give him coal dust, ash or flour and he will tell you anything he knows, one word at a time.
:::
:::

:::secrets
1. Campus Pest Control is a goblin syndicate front. There is no council contract and nobody has ever checked.
2. Barnaby is fully intelligent. Put dust in front of him and he writes.
3. He is allergic to dragon-pepper. One sneeze is a two-foot gout of flame, and the crates are in the same room as his cage.
4. The Boss is selling him at 4:30 to a visiting drow noble, who is a legitimate collector and will hand him straight back if told what he is.
5. Alchemy's counter-agent expires at five. After that it is another week as a cat, and he misses his own deadline.
6. The strong room key hangs on the boiler floor, in plain sight, on a hook labelled *STRONG ROOM*.
:::

**Light:** furnace red, and it does not reach the corners. **The catwalk** on row two runs the length of the complex, ten feet up, and rings under a boot.

:::rooms | AREA | WHAT IS THERE
1 | The service stair | Two goblins in borrowed council tabards. Bored, bribeable at 5 gp, never checked a warrant in their lives.
2 | The steam ducts | Slick catwalk over hissing pipes. DC 12 Acrobatics or 1d6 and a lot of noise. Comes out over the boiler floor.
3 | The boiler floor | Cages, clipboards, the auction slip, and the strong room key on a hook marked *STRONG ROOM*.
4 | The bidding pit | Rusted cages and chain, sunk three feet. From 4:30: **the Boss, 2x Thugs**, a drow noble, eleven bidders.
5 | The strong room | Lot 14 in a cage, and the dragon-pepper crates stacked along the far wall.
:::

:::puzzle Cage Fourteen | area 5 · a lock, a cat, and a crate of pepper
LOOKS: A padlocked iron cage, an orange tabby that will not stop staring at you, and forty pounds of dragon-pepper stacked eight feet away.
SOLVE: DC 14 Thieves' Tools, or the key off the hook in area 3. **Or open a pepper crate near the cage:** Barnaby sneezes, the gout burns through the padlock, and every goblin in the complex comes to look. Any of the three works, and the third one is on fire.
FAIL: Not a dead end. A failed lockpick brings a Thug in one round; the pepper always works and is always loud.
:::

:::box gold | HOW IT ENDS
**Out before five.** Barnaby drinks the counter-agent, is a naked and furious professor by ten past, and the syllabus goes to the printer at 4:55. Five hundred gold, paid on the spot.

**They outbid the drow.** Cheapest ending in the book if somebody has coin. The Boss shakes hands, the noble is gracious, and everybody is home by half four.

**They tell the drow what he is.** She hands him over immediately, apologises to a cat, and reports the syndicate herself. No fee lost, one very useful acquaintance gained.

**Five o'clock passes.** He stays a cat for a week. Alchemy still pays, because the alternative is explaining this to the Dean -- and Barnaby attends every lecture that week, on the desk, watching.
:::

**Cut on the solid line, fold on the dashed one, printed side out.** **Front:** the notice as it hangs, and what the job is, pays and costs. **Back:** the handouts. A document prints as it reads, so cut it out and hand it over; anything meant to be drawn gets an empty frame with the brief along the foot.

:::quest Kobold Scales & Eggs | JOB 1 · LAB MATERIALS NEEDED
GENRE: combat
THEME: wilderness travel, tactical combat
WHERE: Snakebite Crag, two days north
POSTED: Practical Zoology
PAYS: 50 gp
LEVEL: 1–2
DIFFICULTY: Easy
My practical is on Tuesday and I have twenty-six second-years who have watched me hold up the same pickled specimen three years running.

There are live nests up Snakebite Crag. I want a clutch of intact eggs, or failing that a handful of scales -- **fresh**, not shed. I will know the difference.

Fifty gold. Come back alive, please; I cannot face another form this term.
>> A TORN TEXTBOOK PAGE
Fig. 9 -- **KOBOLD CLUTCH, TYPICAL.** Three eggs in section, leathery shell, the yolk sac lettered (b), incubation 40–50 days at vent heat.

*In the margin, in red, pressed hard:* **DO NOT DROP** -- spontaneous arcane detonation may occur.
>> PEN & INK -- THE LECTURER'S NAPKIN MAP
ART: A coffee-stained napkin, torn on one edge, with the road north drawn in biro and an enormous X over Snakebite Crag. Frantic marginal warnings in three different pens -- *watch for falling rocks!*, *bridge??*, *DO NOT take the low path* -- and a ring stain obscuring whatever was written under the X.
!! Give them the page before they leave and the napkin at the trailhead. Between them they carry both warnings the party will ignore.
:::

:::quest Noise Complaint: Dorm Four | JOB 2 · CAMPUS SECURITY
GENRE: social
THEME: investigation, roleplay, no killing
WHERE: East Wing Quadrangle
POSTED: Campus Security
PAYS: 35 gp
LEVEL: 1–2
DIFFICULTY: Easy
Eleven complaints out of Dorm Four this week, and I have three officers who did not sign up for this. We think it is a Zoology student who got too hands-on with his own research.

Find him. **Do not kill him** -- I am putting that in writing. Walk him to Alchemy for the cure and I will sign off whatever you need signed.
>> LEO'S RIPPED JOURNAL PAGE
*Handwriting steady at the top and coming apart by the end.*

Tues. Wolfsbane steeped 6 hrs, ratio 3:1. No effect. Weds. Ratio 5:1, tastes of pennies.

Full moon tonight. wolfsbane ratio wrong. ears itching. everything is so **loud**
>> TEMPORARY SECURITY CLEARANCE
**CAMPUS SECURITY -- TEMPORARY CLEARANCE.** Bearer may bypass dormitory curfew and is authorised the use of **silvered force**. Valid one night. Countersigned, Watch Sergeant.
!! Hand over the clearance at the start. Hold the journal page back until somebody searches Leo's room -- it is the proof he was trying to cure himself.

:::quest The Registration Seal | JOB 3 · STOLEN
GENRE: exploration
THEME: dungeon delve, traps, recovery
WHERE: The Old Archive Vaults, Sub-Level 3
POSTED: Administration & Registrar
PAYS: 60 gp
LEVEL: 1–2
DIFFICULTY: Medium
I cannot process a single enrolment. The master wax seal has gone out of this office, and I watched three Year 3 students go down through the old trapdoor carrying it.

Recover the item. I care very little what else you find down there, or who you find holding it.

Sixty gold, and my office opens again the moment it is on my desk.
>> A FORGED TRANSCRIPT, ABANDONED BY THE TRAPDOOR
**ELDORIA UNIVERSITY -- CONFERRAL OF DEGREE.** This is to certify that the bearer is admitted **Master of Unsanctioned Magic**, with *destinction*, in the faculty of *Abjuraton*.

*The seal at the foot is a potato print. Three names are crossed out above the fourth.*
>> PEN & INK -- OLD VAULT BLUEPRINT, SUB-LEVEL 3
ART: A dusty faded floor plan, drawn in a draughtsman's hand and stamped SUPERSEDED. The scriptorium, the records room and the central platform, with a ruled scale in feet. Pressure plates are marked as small hatched squares, and somebody has pencilled *still live?* beside four of them.
!! The transcript is the whole motive -- give it to them at the trapdoor. The blueprint marks four plates and there are six.
:::

:::quest Archive Stress Test | JOB 4 · PAID PARTICIPANTS
GENRE: combat
THEME: gauntlet, four rooms, survival

POSTED: Dimensional Architecture
PAYS: 75 gp
LEVEL: 1–2
DIFFICULTY: Hard
I need four people willing to be hit. My new planar array wants testing and I will learn nothing whatsoever from a straw dummy.

Four rooms, one at a time, no way back. The Golden Hall Pass is on a plinth at the far end and it is yours if you reach it.

Seventy-five gold on the way out. Sign the waiver first. I would read it.
>> LIABILITY WAIVER -- SIGN BEFORE ENTRY
I, the undersigned, enter the Calibration Chamber of my own free will and release the University from all liability for **dismemberment, disintegration, temporal displacement**, or the loss of equipment, memory, or years.

Signed ........................ Witnessed ........................
>> PARTICIPANT PUNCH-CARD
**FOUR ROOMS. FOUR BOXES. ONE PASS.**

☐ Room 1 ☐ Room 2 ☐ Room 3 ☐ Room 4

*No box, no payment. Do not lose this card.*
!! Make the players actually sign the waiver. Punch or cross a box at the table as each room falls -- it is the whole pacing mechanism.

:::quest Professor Barnaby | JOB 5 · MISSING PERSON (CAT)
GENRE: stealth
THEME: rescue, heist, time crunch
WHERE: Student Centre boiler room
POSTED: Department of Alchemy
PAYS: 500 gp
LEVEL: 1–2
DIFFICULTY: Medium
I need to be precise about this, because it sounds like a joke and it is not one. At eight this morning Professor Barnaby transmuted himself into a cat. At half past, Campus Pest Control took him away in a box.

Campus Pest Control is not a council service.

Get him back before **five**. After five the counter-agent is dead, he stays a cat for a week, and he has a syllabus due.
>> BLACK MARKET AUCTION SLIP
**LOT 14** -- Rare transmuted orange tabby. *Spits fire.* One previous owner, sort of.

Bidding opens **4:30**, Boiler Room B. Cash only. No academics.
>> SYLLABUS DEADLINE TRACKER
**8am** ☐ **9** ☐ **10** ☐ **11** ☐ **12** ☐ **1** ☐ **2** ☐ **3** ☐ **4** ☐ **4:30 AUCTION** ☐ **5pm DEADLINE**

*Cross off an hour for every short rest, bribe, wrong turn or argument.*
!! Put the tracker in the middle of the table and cross the hours off where everybody can see. The auction at 4:30 is half an hour before the deadline on purpose.
:::

:::quest The Dragon of Unpaid Fines | JOB 7 · OVERDUE
GENRE: combat
THEME: monster hunt, water, no fire
WHERE: The Restricted Stacks, deep sub-basement
POSTED: Circulation Desk
PAYS: 100 gp, and all your fines waived
LEVEL: 1–2
DIFFICULTY: Medium
One item. Checked out a hundred and fifty years ago, never returned, fines compounding daily throughout.

The debt has acquired enough physical weight to stand up and walk. It is nesting in my deep archive on a hoard of its own late-fee coppers, and it objects to visitors.

I want the book back. **Intact.** A hundred gold, and I will wipe every fine the four of you owe this library.
>> PEN & INK -- THE ACCORDION RECEIPT
ART: A till receipt of absurd length -- tape three strips of paper together for the table. Ruled columns of dates and pennies compounding down the whole thing, decade by decade, the figures getting longer as they go. At the very bottom, boxed: **TOTAL DUE: 10,420 gp.** Above it, small: *item not yet returned. Interest continues to accrue daily.*
>> PEN & INK -- FOLDED ORIGAMI BOOKMARK
ART: A paper dragon, drawn as a fold-flat pattern the players can genuinely cut out and fold. Printed across the inside faces, so it only reads once unfolded: **SOFTENED BY WATER. SHREDDED BY STEEL.**
!! Hand over the receipt at the desk and let them unfold the bookmark themselves. It is the fight's whole solution and it is worth them finding it.
:::

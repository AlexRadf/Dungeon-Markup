:::pitch
The Senior Staff Lounge went up this morning in a cloud of purple necro-alchemical smoke, and the Dean is threatening whole-house expulsions. Two hundred gold to find out who did it before he throws everybody out.
:::

:::box dark | WHAT IS ACTUALLY HAPPENING -- READ THIS FIRST
**TA Julian Reeve did it, and did not mean to.** Three all-nighters into three hundred midterms, he worked out that fifty hours of marking would not fit before the deadline -- so he bought a joke-shop stink-bomb casing off the Pranksters and stole a pouch of **Blast-Snot powder** from the Necromancy Club, to make the breakroom unusable for a week and win an extension.

Blast-Snot is not inert. It went off properly, took the lounge apart and **melted the safe**, which held the midterms. They are gone. He got what he wanted by accident and is sick about it.

He is hiding in the **Old Library Cellar**, where he panicked again and tried to rebuild the burnt papers with a half-remembered animation cantrip. There is now a **Paper Golem** of three hundred part-regrown midterms lurching about the archive, and it is not guarding him -- it wants to be marked.
:::

:::read STRONG START -- READ IT IN THE CORRIDOR
You are passing the Faculty Lounge when the door comes off its hinges, crosses the corridor and embeds itself in the opposite wall. After it rolls a cloud of purple smoke smelling of sulphur, burnt chicory, and faintly of cheap coffee.
:::

:::secrets
1. The device was a stink bomb. Nobody was meant to be hurt and nobody was.
2. The Necromancy Club lost a pouch of Blast-Snot yesterday and has not reported it, because reporting it is a suspension.
3. The Pranksters have a cast-iron alibi -- all four in detention -- and are furious that somebody out-pranked them.
4. The midterms were in the breakroom safe. The safe melted. They are gone.
5. Julian has been awake for three days and had done sixty of three hundred papers.
6. The Paper Golem wants to be marked, not to fight. A grade written on the top sheet ends it.
:::

:::box red | THE FOUR ENCOUNTERS
**1. The wreckage** *(investigation)* DC 12 Investigation for the residue, DC 10 to find the casing fragments. The Dean is in the corridor demanding names and getting in the way.

**2. The three factions** *(social, in any order)* Necromancers want it kept quiet, the Frat want somebody else blamed, the Pranksters want credit for a prank they did not commit. All three will trade information for a promise.

**3. The cellar** *(the Paper Golem)* It comes at whoever is holding a pen. It is not trying to kill anybody, but it weighs three hundred essays.

**4. Julian** *(social, and the actual end of the job)* Behind the stacks, awake for three days, entirely ready to confess to anybody who asks him gently.
:::

:::cols 2
:::stat Paper Golem | CR 2 | AC 12 | HP 40 | SPD 20 ft | STR +3 | DEX -1 | CON +2 | INT -5 | WIS -1 | CHA -5
**Smother** +5, 5 ft: 9 (2d6+2) bludgeoning, and a Medium or smaller target is grappled (escape DC 13) under three hundred sheets of paper.
**Flying pen** +4, 30 ft: 4 (1d6+1) piercing. It has one and it throws it, repeatedly.
**Vulnerable** fire -- which destroys the midterms and the evidence with them.
**Mark it and it stops.** A grade written on the top sheet, in ink, ends the golem instantly. Any grade. It does not check.
> Area: the paper archive · one · the fight is entirely optional and it takes one action to avoid

:::npc TA Julian Reeve | Faculty of Alchemy · three days awake · caught
WANT: To have not done this. Failing that, for somebody else to decide what happens next.
VOICE: Too fast, then stops mid-sentence. Apologises for things that are not his fault.
BREAK: He did sixty papers out of three hundred and worked out the arithmetic at four in the morning.
LINE: Fifty hours. I did the sum four times. There aren't fifty hours between now and Friday.
USE: He will confess to the Dean, in writing, if somebody goes in with him. He will not go in alone.

:::box gold | HOW IT ENDS
**They hand Julian in.** Two hundred gold. He is dismissed, the Necromancy Club is suspended for the missing pouch, and three hundred students resit in a fortnight.

**They hand Julian in, and go in with him.** Same two hundred gold, and the Dean -- who has also graded three hundred papers in his life -- reduces it to a written warning and a rota review. Julian keeps his post and owes the party everything.

**They blame a faction.** The Dean expels a house on the party's word. It is quick, it is paid, and it is wrong, and one of those students will remember the party's faces for a long time.

**They say nothing.** The Dean expels all three houses on Friday. Nobody sat the midterms anyway, because the midterms melted.
:::

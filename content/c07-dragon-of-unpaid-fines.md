:::pitch
A book called *The Economics of Accumulation* went out a hundred and fifty years ago and never came back. The fines kept compounding. The Head Librarian wants the book returned intact, will pay a hundred gold, and will waive every fine the party has ever run up.
:::

:::box dark | WHAT IS ACTUALLY HAPPENING -- READ THIS FIRST
The library runs an **auto-enforcement enchantment**: unpaid fees are converted into physical mass and welded onto the overdue item. A hundred and fifty years of daily compound interest gave *The Economics of Accumulation* so much mass that it stood up. It is now an origami dragon of folded overdue notices with the tome for a heart, nesting in the sub-basement on ten thousand gold in late-fee coppers.

**It does not want to fight.** It is compulsively, miserably obsessed with guarding the hoard, and it attacks anything that touches the coins or the book. Left alone in a room it fusses over the pile.

**Water and ink soften it.** Wet parchment is limp parchment: −2 AC, and the breath fails entirely for a round. There is a leaking pipe on the map for exactly this reason. **Fire deals double** -- and burning the book forfeits the bounty and lands the party the 10,420 gp fine instead, which Grimms will pursue.

**It ends when the tome is returned to the desk.** Stamped in, the debt clears, the enchantment releases, and the dragon comes apart into a drift of loose paper in the returns trolley.
:::

:::read STRONG START -- READ IT AT THE CIRCULATION DESK
Head Librarian Grimms slaps a ledger down in front of you. It is the size of a paving slab, it is vibrating, and the compound-interest runes along its spine are glowing a hot arterial red. "One item," she says. "One hundred and fifty years. It has stopped being an item." Somewhere a long way below your feet, something turns over, and it sounds like a hundred pages at once.
:::

:::cols 2
:::beats
1. **The stacks.** Fifty-foot shelves, rolling ladders that drift on their own, and aisles that are not where the plan says. DC 12 Survival or Investigation to keep a line; failure costs twenty minutes and a random encounter.
2. **The slips.** A swarm of overdue notices comes down an aisle at head height. Paper cuts, no real danger, and they are *herding* the party toward the vault.
3. **The pipe.** A leaking magical water pipe over the flooded gallery. Anybody who notices it has the fight solved before it starts.
4. **The hoard.** Ten thousand coppers, and the dragon on top of them, and the tome visible in its chest.
5. **Parley or fight.** It can be talked at -- it understands debt and nothing else -- or soaked, or killed. All three work.
6. **The return.** Up the stairs, tome on the counter, stamp comes down.
:::

:::npc Head Librarian Grimms | Circulation Desk · thirty years · terrifying
WANT: The item back. Intact. She has not said "please" since the war.
VOICE: Clipped, quiet, uses the full title of everything.
BREAK: She has known what was down there for eleven years and has not told the Dean.
LINE: The fine is not a punishment. The fine is arithmetic.
USE: She waives all fines on return, and she means it. She will also charge for water damage.
:::
:::

:::secrets
1. The book was checked out by a wizard student, Perrin Vale, who dropped out to become a pirate. His card is still in the pocket.
2. The enchantment turns unpaid fees into physical armour. The dragon is literally made of what is owed.
3. Water or ink softens the parchment: **−2 AC**, and no breath weapon on the round it is soaked.
4. Fire deals double damage -- but if the tome burns, the party owes 10,420 gp and Grimms will collect.
5. It does not want to fight. It wants the hoard counted and safe, and it cannot stop.
6. Returning the tome to the desk clears the debt and unmakes the dragon on the spot. Nobody has to die here.
:::

:::item Paper Dragon Scale | Wondrous, common -- the hoard
One folded scale, dry and stiff as card. Unfolded and thrown, it is a single casting of *feather fall*. Once only, and it cannot be refolded. In the tome's back pocket, Perrin Vale's borrower card: *gone to sea, sorry about the book.*
:::

:::puzzle Softened By Water, Shredded By Steel | anywhere below · the bookmark says it outright
LOOKS: A folded bookmark from the returns trolley. Unfolded, printed inside: **SOFTENED BY WATER. SHREDDED BY STEEL.**
SOLVE: Get water on it. The pipe at area 5 fills the bucket from area 1; a soaked dragon is AC 15 with no breath. Fighting it in the flooded gallery does the same job for free.
FAIL: Not a dead end, just the hard version -- dry in its own vault it is AC 17 with a 7d6 cone, a real fight at level 2.
:::

:::box gold | HOW IT ENDS
**They return the tome.** Grimms stamps it in. The debt clears, the enchantment lets go, and whatever is left of the dragon comes apart into loose paper in the trolley. 100 gp, every fine the party owes wiped, and a clean campus record.

**They talk it down.** It understands one thing: the debt. Show it a ledger marked *settled* and it puts the book down itself and folds up quietly.

**They burn it.** Fastest fight in the book, and the tome goes with it: the party owes 10,420 gp and Grimms bills them that afternoon. **They run.** It stays, fifty feet under a building full of students, and the fines keep compounding.
:::

:::pitch
Semester registration is frozen. The master wax seal -- legally required on every piece of enrolment paperwork -- has been stolen, and Admin saw Year 3 students going down into the old ruins under campus. Sixty gold to get it back.
:::

:::box dark | WHAT IS ACTUALLY HAPPENING -- READ THIS FIRST
Three Year 3 students -- **Vasso, Pell and Quint** -- failed Advanced Abjuration and cannot graduate. They took the seal to stamp themselves forged transcripts. They are not villains. They are twenty, desperate, and monumentally out of their depth.

The old vaults were built on top of a **sunken temple to a god of knowledge**, and the university's own seal is the key to the temple's central defence ward. Stamping a document down here woke the ward up.

**An Animated Armour is now hunting them**, and they are stranded on the records platform with a ten-foot drop onto iron spikes on every side and the armour patrolling the ring. They have been up there four hours. Pell is hurt.

**The seal stops the armour.** Present it openly to the construct and it stands down and returns to its alcove, because the bearer of the seal is by definition authorised. The party will almost certainly try to fight the thing first, and that is fine -- it is a CR 1 and they will win -- but the seal was always the off switch.
:::

:::read STRONG START -- READ IT IN THE ADMIN OFFICE
Registrar Corvus pulls a tapestry off the wall to show you a trapdoor nobody has opened since before he was appointed. Cold air comes up out of it smelling of wet stone and old ink. Somewhere a long way down, something metal takes a step, stops, and takes another.
:::

:::cols 2
:::beats
1. **The trapdoor.** A forged transcript dropped in the hurry down. Read it and the whole motive is on the page.
2. **The flooded scriptorium.** Ankle-deep ink-water, crumbling lecterns, and pressure plates under the surface. The blueprint marks four. There are six.
3. **The glyph door.** Sealed, and it opens for anybody who can read the temple's one word. Or for anybody holding the seal.
4. **The records vault.** The armour on patrol in the ring, three students stranded on the platform, and a lot of shouting once they see lamplight.
5. **Get them down.** Ten feet, spikes, one injured. A rope, a plank, or a very good jump.
6. **Back up the stair** with the seal, three undergraduates, and a decision about what to tell Corvus.
:::

:::npc Registrar Corvus | Administration · no-nonsense, and not unkind
WANT: The seal back and registration reopened. He genuinely does not care who took it.
VOICE: Bureaucratic shorthand. Says "the item" rather than "the seal", every time.
BREAK: He failed Advanced Abjuration twice himself, in 1174, and has never mentioned it to anybody.
LINE: Recover the item. What you recover alongside the item is your affair.
USE: That line is permission. He will process three late resit applications without comment if they are on his desk by Friday.
:::
:::

:::secrets
1. The Year 3s are not evil -- they failed Advanced Abjuration and need transcripts to graduate.
2. The vaults sit on a sunken temple to a god of knowledge. The architecture is much older than the university.
3. The seal is the key to the temple's central ward. Stamping anything down here woke the armour.
4. Show the armour the seal and it stands down. That is the intended answer and nobody has to be hit.
5. The blueprint marks four pressure plates. There are six. The two unmarked ones are in the doorway.
6. Corvus failed the same module twice and will quietly process resits if anybody asks him to.
:::

:::item Scroll of Knock | Scroll, uncommon -- a lectern in area 2
Dry, in an oilcloth, on the one lectern still standing. Older than the university and still perfectly good.
:::

**Light:** none, and the ink-water swallows lantern light. **The `:` ring** around the platform is a ten-foot drop onto iron spikes: 1d6 falling plus 2d4 piercing, and climbing out is DC 12.

:::rooms | AREA | WHAT IS THERE
1 | The trapdoor stair | Down from behind the tapestry. A forged transcript dropped on the third step.
2 | The flooded scriptorium | Ankle-deep ink-water, stone lecterns, **six pressure plates** under the surface. DC 13 Perception each; a triggered plate fires a glyph bolt, 2d6 force, DC 13 Dex for half.
3 | The glyph door | One word cut into it in temple script. DC 14 Arcana or Religion to read, or hold up the seal and it opens itself.
4 | The vault ring | Where the **Animated Armour** patrols. Ten feet wide the whole way round, spikes on the inside edge.
5 | The records platform | Three Year 3s, one injured, four hours stranded, and the seal in Vasso's coat pocket.
:::

**Authorised bearer.** Show it the seal in an open hand: it stops, salutes, and walks back to its alcove. Permanently.
**It will not enter the water.** It patrols the ring and the ring only.

**Dagger** +3, 5 ft: 3 (1d4+1) piercing. **Shield** (1/day, Pell): +5 AC against one attack.
**Vasso has the seal** and hands it over the moment somebody offers to help rather than arrest.
**Pell is at 3 hp** from the spikes and cannot climb.
> Area 5 · x3 · they are the rescue, not the encounter

:::puzzle Six Plates, Four Marked | area 2 · the blueprint is out of date
LOOKS: Ankle-deep black water, and a faded floor plan with four small hatched squares pencilled *still live?*
SOLVE: Four are where the blueprint says. **The two it does not show are in the doorway**, one under each jamb, added when the door was rehung. Probe with a pole, weigh them, or cross along the lecterns. Anybody who notices the doorway is blank on the plan finds them free.
FAIL: 2d6 force and every glyph in the room lights up, which brings the armour to the door and holds it there.
:::

:::box gold | HOW IT ENDS
**Seal up, students out.** Show the armour the seal, rope the three of them off the platform, and walk everybody up. Sixty gold, registration reopens, and Corvus processes three resits without a word.

**They fight the armour.** It is CR 1 and they will win, and it takes four rounds they could have spent getting Pell off the spikes.

**They hand the students in.** Also sixty gold. All three are sent down, the forged transcript goes in the file, and the party has made three enemies who will graduate anyway in two years.

**They leave without the seal.** Registration stays frozen, nobody enrols, and by Friday the whole university is the party's problem.
:::

:::pitch
The wards on the quiet study floor reset at first bell, and between now and then the floor is not the floor. The night custodian has done six of these on his own with a mop and a set of keys, and is paying fifty gold out of his own pocket for company.
!! THEME -- six hours of holding a line, and the way to end it early is to accept a returned library book.
:::

:::read WHERE IT STARTS -- READ IT WITH THE DOORS ALREADY LOCKED
Six hours to first bell. The quiet study floor has stopped being a room with windows in it: it is a keep, and past the brass line there is a plain of bones running out considerably further than the building can possibly go. The desk is behind you. Something out there is already walking.
:::

One room, six hours, one line you do not cross. Twelve brass segments, cut when the library was built -- and **segment 9 was lifted for cabling three years ago and never re-cut.**

:::rooms | AREA | WHAT IS THERE
1 | The desk | The objective. Everything is measured from it. The overdue notice is in the tray, already filled in, and it requires a witness.
2 | The line | Brass, twelve segments, eleven of which hold. Waves break on it at hours one to four and cannot cross.
3 | The stacks | Cover, choke points, and things you must not shelve. Six incident forms in the tray, none countersigned, every one about segment 9.
9 | Segment 9 | Not cut, not warded. **At hour five something comes through here**, behind you. Re-cuttable mid-fight with a chisel and somebody covering.
4 | The plain | Out there, further out than the building goes. The chariot is on it, it is made of the book, and it leaves when the book does.
:::

:::secrets
1. The Dread-Lord is a borrower. He has come to give a book back six nights running and been refused every time, and he is starting to take it personally.
2. Tobias refuses because the notice says *do not accept without a witness*, and in eleven years of nights he has never had one.
3. The wards keep him out. They also stop him handing it over, which is genuinely the only thing he wants.
4. Accept the return, with a witness, and the siege stops mid-wave. Everything on the plain simply puts its spears down.
5. The book is what makes the plain. The library knows and wants it back rather badly.
6. The closure notice says asbestos. There is no asbestos. Somebody in Estates picked a word nobody argues with.
:::

:::beats
1. **Hour four: elites reach the line**, which is where you have been standing all night.
2. **Hour five: something comes through segment 9**, behind you, between you and the desk.
3. **Hour six: the chariot crosses**, unhurried, because nothing is stopping it now.
4. **First bell.** The wards reset with the floor full, and lock it in with everything on it.
:::

:::box red | TROUBLE
- One wave an hour, six hours, and the desk cannot be left for any of them.
- Elites at hour four, which is where a party that has been fighting properly starts to lose.
- Segment 9, the moment anything notices it, and something notices at hour five.
- The chariot at hour six, which does not fight and does not have to.
:::

:::box gold | WAYS OUT
- Hold six hours and let dawn do it. First bell resets the wards and it all goes.
- Re-cut segment 9 mid-fight with a chisel, a mop handle and somebody covering.
- Take the return, witness it, stamp it, and end the siege in the middle of a wave.
:::

:::cols 2
:::npc Tobias Ell, night custodian | Eleven years of nights · a mop, a ring of keys, fifty gold of his own
WANT: Somebody else in the room. That is all of it, and he is embarrassed about it.
VOICE: Quiet, apologetic, and utterly immovable on the two rules.
BREAK: Six refusals, because the form says witness and he has never had one.
LINE: I'm not being difficult. It says witness. I've read it six times. It says witness.
USE: He is the witness the notice needs and you are the witness he needs. That is the whole solution.
:::

:::puzzle The Return | the desk · a form, a witness, a stamp
LOOKS: A borrower at the line holding a book out, and a notice in the tray saying do not accept without a witness.
SOLVE: One person at the desk, one witnessing, one stamping. It takes a single round and ends the siege mid-wave.
FAIL: Refusing again is not a dead end. It is hour six, and hour six is the chariot.
:::

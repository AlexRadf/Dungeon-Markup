!! THEME -- six hours of holding a line, and the way to end it is to accept a returned book.

:::read ON THE BOARD -- AS POSTED
The wards on the quiet study floor reset at first bell. Between now and then the floor is not the floor.

I'm one man with a mop and a set of keys and I've done six of these nights on my own.

Stand at the circulation desk until dawn. Don't cross the brass line. Don't shelve anything. Whatever asks you to, don't.
:::

:::cols 2
:::box plain | ON THE WAY THERE
The quiet study floor has been closed to students since spring and the closure notice says asbestos. The custodian has worked nights eleven years and has never once called security.

The brass strip across the floor was laid when the library was built. Facilities lifted a section of it three years ago for cabling and the work order was never closed. Six night shifts, six incident forms, none of them countersigned. Dawn is first bell.
:::

:::box dark | HANDOUT -- AN OVERDUE NOTICE, ALREADY FILLED IN, IN THE CUSTODIAN'S TRAY
BORROWER: **unrecorded**. ITEM: one, uncatalogued. DUE: **4 January, four hundred and eleven years ago**. FINE ACCRUED: not calculable by this system.

NOTE: borrower has attended in person on six previous occasions. **Do not accept the return without a witness.**
:::
:::

:::box plain | HANDOUT -- PEN & INK -- WARD PLATE
The diagram cut into the brass strip, reproduced as a technical plate in the library's maintenance manual. Concentric rings, dense even hatching between them, an inner band of close-set script, radial marks numbered 1 to 12. Draw it precisely and coldly -- ruled compass work, no flourish. Its authority is the point: this is a plate about masonry, not magic. One thing is wrong with it. Segment 9 is drawn broken, and a later, shakier hand has written beside it: *lifted for cabling 3 yrs ago. Never re-cut. Reported 4x.*
:::

:::secrets
1. The Dread-Lord is a borrower. He has come to return a book, six nights running, and been refused every time.
2. The custodian has refused six times because the notice requires a witness and he has never had one.
3. The wards keep him out. They also stop him giving it back, which is the only thing he wants.
4. Accept the return at the desk, with a witness, and the siege ends mid-wave. Everything out there simply stops.
5. The book is what is making the plain. The library knows this and wants it back more than anyone.
6. Segment 9 was lifted for cabling three years ago and never re-cut. It has been reported four times.
7. The closure notice says asbestos. There is no asbestos. Somebody in Estates chose a word nobody argues with.
8. Six incident forms, six nights, none countersigned -- because countersigning one would make it real.
:::

:::box red | TROUBLE
- One wave an hour, six hours, and the desk cannot be abandoned for any of them.
- Elites at hour four, which is where a party that has been fighting properly starts to lose.
- Segment 9, the moment anything out there notices it -- and something notices at hour five.
- The chariot at hour six, which does not fight and does not need to.
:::

:::box gold | WAYS OUT
- Hold six hours and let dawn do it. First bell resets the wards and everything goes.
- Re-cut segment 9 mid-fight, with a mop handle, a chisel and somebody covering.
- Take the return, witness it, stamp it, and the siege stops in the middle of a wave.
:::

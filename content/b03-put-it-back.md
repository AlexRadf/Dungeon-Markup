:::pitch
Beast Husbandry took an egg off a griffon's nest with every form correctly filed, and has spent a month going quietly off the idea. It goes back up four hundred feet of crag before the mother finishes counting. It is also nine days from hatching, which nobody has written down anywhere.
!! THEME -- a heist run backwards, uphill, against a clock somebody rounded down to make the job look cheap.
:::

:::read WHERE IT STARTS -- READ IT AT THE BOTTOM OF THE CLIMB
The crate is roped to a pony and the pony loathes it, in the way animals loathe a thing they can hear. Four hundred feet of crag above you, the nest two-thirds of the way up, and not one bird in the sky -- so she is out on her circuit, and nobody has the faintest idea how far along.
:::

:::beats
1. **The bottom of the crag.** No bird up there. Nobody can tell you how far into her circuit she is, and the notice cheerfully says four days.
2. **The scree.** Loose, loud, and the only way up in daylight. Somebody has to carry the crate, and every third step goes.
3. **The old nests.** Eleven years of bones sorted by season. Anybody who counts them works out the circuit is six days, not four, and that Husbandry knew.
4. **The crack opens.** Four rounds of blood heat and no inverting, on a rope, four hundred feet up, while something inside starts pushing.
5. **The male.** Smaller than her, starving, and absolutely not leaving that ledge. This is the fight, if there is one, and it can be ended by holding the egg up.
6. **The nest, and down again**, four eggs and a gap the exact shape of what you are carrying -- with a chick, or without one, and the pony either is or is not where you left it.
:::

:::cols 2
:::place The Scree | loose, loud, and the only daylight route
- Every third step goes, and the noise carries the full four hundred feet.
- Nothing to tie to for the first hundred and fifty. No anchors, no line, no mercy.
- From here you can see the notch, which means the notch can see you.
:::

:::place The Old Nests | three abandoned ledges, eleven years of bones
- The lining has been robbed out recently, in a hurry, and taken upward.
- A half-plucked goat, three days dead, dragged up by something that could not carry it far.
- Bones in eleven neat seasonal layers. Count them and you have the circuit.
:::

:::place The Nest | four eggs, one gap, and the shape of the gap
- Fresh lining, stolen from below, packed the way a bird packs when it is short-handed.
- Nine days of down lying undisturbed right across the ledge.
- The gap is warm. Something has been sitting patiently over a space where nothing is.
:::
:::

:::secrets
1. The egg is nine days from hatching. The crack opens on the climb, not before, and the chick's own hatching-spur made it.
2. Her circuit is six days, not four. Husbandry wrote four on the notice to price the job cheap.
3. The male is feeding four eggs single-handed. He is starving, he will not leave the ledge, and he is the only reason nothing has been eaten yet.
4. Ossoway took the egg on Draconics' written instruction. Draconics now denies instructing it, and the instruction is still in his coat pocket.
5. A hatched chick imprints on whoever it sees first, permanently. Husbandry knows this and has quietly budgeted for it.
6. Nobody at Stonehaven has lost a lamb in nine days, which is not good news at all -- it means she is somewhere else.
:::

+++

:::cols 2
:::box red | TROUBLE
- The climb, with the crate. Four hundred feet, and it must not be inverted.
- The male, who is smaller than her and much worse, because he cannot afford to lose.
- The hairline crack. If it opens on the rope, somebody is holding a hatching griffon one-handed.
- Her, back early, which is exactly the same problem as her being back on time.
:::

:::box gold | WAYS OUT
- Put it back before the crack opens. The job as written, and the hardest way to do it.
- Let it hatch and carry the chick up anyway. It imprints. That is a problem you get to keep.
- Hand the male the egg. He will take it, and he will remember you for eleven years.
:::
:::

:::beats
1. **She finishes the circuit and lands**, and she is a bird that can count to four.
2. **She works down the pass**, and takes every sheet off every washing line in Stonehaven.
3. **She follows the van's smell to Oakhaven**, because it is the last thing she smelled on her own ledge.
4. **She finds the loading bay** -- inside the town, doors open, eleven in the morning, in front of everybody.
:::

:::cols 2
:::stat The Male | CR 2 | AC 12 | HP 45 | SPD 30 ft, fly 80 ft | STR +3 | DEX +2 | CON +2 | INT -4 | WIS +1 | CHA -2
**Beak** +5, 5 ft: 8 (1d8+3) piercing. **Two talons** +5, 5 ft: 6 (1d6+3) slashing each.
**Will not leave the shelf.** He fights within 20 ft of the nest and nowhere else, ever, for anything.
**Starving.** Disadvantage on his second attack each round, and he will absolutely take a thrown goat.
**Give him the egg.** Presenting it openly ends the fight on the spot. He is not defending a territory.
> The ledge · one bird · the only fight that is actually likely

:::cols 2
:::puzzle The Hairline Crack | on the rope · four rounds, then it is a chick
LOOKS: A hair of a line across the shell, and it was not there when you roped the crate on.
SOLVE: Blood heat, no inverting. Somebody climbs one-handed, or it goes up on a second line while somebody keeps it warm against them.
FAIL: It hatches on the face. Four feet of wet, extremely loud griffon, imprinted on whoever is holding it.
:::

:::roll d6 | COMPLICATION

2 Scree goes. DC 13 DEX or slide 20 ft.
3 The crate inverts. Nobody admits it.
4 Wind straight down the face.
5 A shepherd, following the van's tracks.
6 The middle ledge wakes up.
:::

:::item Ossoway's Instruction | Paper, one sheet -- his coat, or the loading bay
A Draconics requisition, signed and dated, ordering the collection of one viable egg from the Roost. Draconics has denied issuing it twice in writing. It is worth a great deal more than sixty gold to Beast Husbandry, and rather more than that to the Proctor.
:::

:::valve
If it's dragging: the crack opens a quarter-inch and something inside moves against the shell.
If it's going fast: a shadow crosses the face twice and does not come back.
Cliffhanger: they get to the bottom and the crate is warm, and light, and open.
:::

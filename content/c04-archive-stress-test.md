:::pitch
Dimensional Architecture needs live combatants to stress-test the Arcane Library's new planar security array. Four rooms, one at a time, and a Golden Hall Pass at the end. Seventy-five gold on return. The waiver is not a joke and they will make you sign it.
:::

:::box dark | WHAT IS ACTUALLY HAPPENING -- READ THIS FIRST
It is a real test of a real security array, and **the damage is real.** "Simulated" means the monsters are constructs and the rooms reset afterwards. It does not mean anybody is pretending.

The whole array runs off **one power crystal, in Room 4, behind the construct.** Break it and every room shuts down at once -- doors open, monsters stop, test over. This is not cheating. It is the single most useful result the department could get, and Vance in Dimensional Architecture will pay the full fee for it and buy the party a drink.

**Room 2's lava is an illusion.** It is twenty feet of empty air over crash padding, dressed with light and heat. A DC 13 Investigation or any *detect magic* sees through it; anybody who walks straight across takes nothing at all. Falling in costs 2d6 bludgeoning and a great deal of dignity.

**Room 3 is a genuine rest station.** The Study Bell on the plinth casts a five-minute sanctuary field -- nothing can enter, and it is long enough for a short rest. It rings once and only once.
:::

:::read STRONG START -- READ IT AS THEY STEP THROUGH
The portal does not put you down. It puts you *out*, six feet up, over a floor of drifting stone tiles, and the safety door seals itself behind you with a sound like a bank vault. A cheerful brass voice from nowhere in particular says: "Room one of four. Good luck. Please do not damage the desks."
:::

:::cols 2
:::beats
1. **Room 1: the swarm.** Flying book swarms and floating desks. The desks are cover and the voice has just told everybody not to break them, which is the joke.
2. **Room 2: the crossing.** A field of "lava" and five stepping stones. Somebody will look at it properly, or somebody will jump.
3. **Room 3: the bell.** A plinth, a bell, and five minutes of absolute safety if anybody thinks to ring it.
4. **Room 4: the construct.** A silent rune-walled arena, one Helmed Horror, and a crystal humming in an alcove behind it.
5. **The pass, or the crystal.** Beat the construct and take the pass off the plinth, or break the crystal and walk out through open doors.
6. **Punch the card.** Four boxes. No box, no payment -- so cross them off at the table as they go.
:::

:::npc Adjunct Vance, Dimensional Architecture | Runs the array · delighted by failure
WANT: Data. Any data. A party that breaks the array is worth more to her than one that completes it.
VOICE: Brisk, cheerful, takes notes while you are still bleeding.
BREAK: This is the fourth test. The first three groups all died in Room 2, from the fall, and she has not told anybody.
LINE: Oh, *marvellous.* Nobody has done that. Hold still, I want the exact time.
USE: She pays for the crystal, the pass, or a corpse. She is not sinister, she is just an academic.
:::
:::

:::secrets
1. Every room runs off one crystal in Room 4. Break it and the whole array shuts down at once.
2. The Room 2 lava is illusory -- twenty feet of air over padding. DC 13 Investigation, or just walk out onto it.
3. Three previous groups died in Room 2. All three fell. None of them checked.
4. Destroying the floating desks in Room 1 removes the only cover from the swarms. The voice warns them for a reason.
5. The Study Bell in Room 3 gives a forced five-minute sanctuary. It works once and it is enough for a short rest.
6. Vance pays the full fee for a broken array. Finding the flaw *is* the stress test.
:::

:::item The Golden Hall Pass | Wondrous, uncommon -- area 5, on the plinth
Unrestricted access to the Restricted Library, in perpetuity, signed and sealed. It also opens the Reading Room after hours, which is worth more.
:::

**Light:** even and sourceless. **The doors** open only when the room is cleared, and never backwards.

:::rooms | AREA | WHAT IS THERE
1 | The portal chamber | You land six feet up on drifting tiles, the door seals, the punch-card is issued.
2 | Room one -- the swarm | **3x Flying Book Swarms**, four floating desks. The desks are three-quarters cover and the swarms cannot cross them.
3 | Room two -- the crossing | Five stones over molten fluid. **It is an illusion:** 20 ft of air over padding, 2d6 to fall, nothing else.
4 | Room three -- the rest | A plinth and a Study Bell. Ring it: five minutes of sanctuary, once, enough for a short rest.

**Swarm.** Resists physical damage. Cannot enter a space occupied by a desk.
**No cover, no chance.** A party that has smashed the desks fights all three in the open, which is the lesson.
> Area 2 · x3 · they come in one at a time, a round apart

:::stat Stone Golem Construct | CR 4 | AC 18 | HP 60 | SPD 30 ft | STR +4 | DEX +0 | CON +4 | INT -3 | WIS +0 | CHA -3
**Two slams** +6, 5 ft: 9 (1d10+4) bludgeoning each.
**Immune** poison, psychic, charmed, frightened. **Resists** non-magical weapons.
**Tethered.** It never steps in front of the crystal alcove -- a gap anybody watching spots in two rounds.
**It stops with the crystal.** Break that and it goes inert mid-swing.
> Area 5 · one · 60 hp rather than the usual 90, because this one is a training model
:::
:::

:::puzzle The Lava That Is Not | area 3 · twenty feet of air and a light show
LOOKS: A wide bath of molten orange, five stone steps across it, and heat you can genuinely feel on your face.
SOLVE: The heat is real and the fluid is not. DC 13 Investigation from the edge, *detect magic*, or throwing anything heavier than a coin in -- it goes straight through and lands with a thump. Then walk across the floor and ignore the steps entirely.
FAIL: Jumping the stones is DC 12 Athletics each, four jumps. A miss is a 20 ft fall for 2d6 bludgeoning onto padding, and then a climb back up.
:::

:::box gold | HOW IT ENDS
**All four rooms, pass in hand.** Seventy-five gold, the Golden Hall Pass, and Vance writes the array up as sound.

**They break the crystal.** Every door in the chamber opens at once and everything stops. Vance pays in full, cancels the next three tests, and names the flaw after whoever found it.

**They walk across the lava.** The fastest completion on record. Vance goes very quiet, then asks them to do it again while she watches.

**Somebody falls and they carry on anyway.** Fine -- it is 2d6 and a bruise. The point is that three groups before them thought it was death and behaved accordingly.
:::

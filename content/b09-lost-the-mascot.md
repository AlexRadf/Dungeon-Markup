:::pitch
The Forestry Club's specimen has outgrown greenhouse three, taken the university mascot off the front lawn, and gone through the koi pond with him. No water was displaced. The Club would like this handled quietly, inside the Club, in the next ten rounds, please.
!! THEME -- an elderly boar in a ribbon, a plant that has bitten off far more than it can chew, and a clock you count out loud.
:::

:::read WHERE IT STARTS -- READ IT AT THE POND, AND START COUNTING
The reflection in the koi pond is not the sky. You go in dry and come out dry on the other side, into heat, under a closed canopy, and the whole jungle is one plant. You can still hear him. He sounds furious. He sounds a very long way in.
:::

:::beats
1. **Through the pond.** Dry in, dry out, into a glasshouse August under a shut canopy -- and it has already started closing behind you.
2. **Rounds one to three: the drag.** A channel torn inward through the moss, six feet wide, going one way. It is the trap and it is also the answer.
3. **Round four: the beds.** Acid hydras both sides, and the only path runs between them. Going round costs three rounds you have not got.
4. **Round five: the ribbon** comes free in the crop and floats up through the fluid where everybody can see it.
5. **Rounds six to nine.** Heat, salt, or acid borrowed from the beds, applied to six feet of trunk by somebody with a free hand and somebody else buying them the time.
6. **Round ten: it closes for the season.** Not dramatically -- it simply shuts, with him inside, until spring. The table has been counting and knows exactly whose round it was.
:::

:::cols 2
:::place The Drag | a channel torn inward through the moss
- Six feet wide, going one way, and something heavy went along it this morning.
- Acid scarring down both edges, older than the drag itself.
- Dead straight between the hydra beds, because whatever made it does not care about hydras.
:::

:::place The Crop | six feet of swollen trunk at the base of the tree
- Warm to the hand, and moving, slowly, on a count of about nine.
- A ribbon in university colours visible through the wall at four feet.
- Underneath it all, a noise like a very large animal complaining into a pillow.
:::

:::place The Canopy | the roof, the walls, and the way home
- Shut overhead in every direction, and closing further one round at a time since you arrived.
- It feeds the hydras, it grew the drag, and it owns the pond. It is all one plant.
- When it shuts over the water, the water is still on the Botany lawn.
:::
:::

:::secrets
1. The Titan cannot be killed in ten rounds by anything the party is carrying. Not with a good plan. Not at all.
2. It can be made to *open* -- heat, salt, or enough acid borrowed from its own hydras.
3. Bartholomew is entirely unhurt, extremely cross, and the smell will never fully come out of the ribbon.
4. Ossoway fed it rabbits in week three and then stopped writing down what he fed it in week four.
5. The pond has belonged to the plant rather than to the university for about a year now.
6. The whole jungle is one plant: the hydras are fed by it, the canopy is it, the drag is where it moved.
:::

:::beats
1. **Round ten: it shuts for the season**, with him inside, and opens again in April.
2. **The canopy closes over the pond**, and the pond is on the Botany lawn.
3. **Something comes back through the water at the Botany end**, and it is not a boar.
:::

:::cols 2
:::box red | TROUBLE
- Hydras spit acid and regrow the head you just cut, because the canopy grows it back for them.
- The canopy closing behind you, one round at a time, from the moment you arrive.
- Ten rounds. Count them out loud at the table. That is the entire job.
- The Titan does not have to move to win. It only has to not open.
:::

:::box gold | WAYS OUT
- Cut him out and run, which costs somebody a forearm and is still the quickest way.
- Make it spit: heat, salt, or its own hydras' acid, delivered to the right six feet of trunk.
- Seal the pond and lose him until spring, which the Club will accept and never once forgive.
:::
:::

:::cols 2
:::stat Bed Hydra | CR 4 | AC 15 | HP 82 | SPD 20 ft, swim 30 ft | STR +4 | DEX +1 | CON +4 | INT -4 | WIS +0 | CHA -3
**Three bites** +6, 10 ft: 8 (1d10+4) piercing each, one per head.
**Acid spit** +5, 30 ft: 14 (4d6) acid. Bottle it and you are holding what opens the crop.
**Regrow.** Sever a head and it is back at the start of its next turn unless the stump is burned.
**Fed.** Fed by the canopy, cannot leave its bed. Walk past and take the damage.
> The path inward · x2, a bed each side · going round costs three rounds
:::

:::stat The Titan | CR 8 | AC 17 | HP 260 | SPD 0 ft | STR +7 | DEX -3 | CON +6 | INT -4 | WIS +2 | CHA -2
**It cannot be killed tonight.** 260 hit points and ten rounds. This block exists to make that arithmetic honest at the table.
**Two vines** +9, 30 ft: 16 (3d8+7) bludgeoning and grappled (DC 17). Drops you in the crop at 20 ft of movement.
**The crop.** A grappled creature is swallowed at the end of its next turn: blind, six feet down, 10 (3d6) acid a round.
**It opens for heat, salt, or acid.** Any one of the three on the crop wall: it spits everything out and shuts again in a round.
> The trunk · one plant · the jungle, the beds and the pond are all part of it
:::
:::

:::cols 2
:::npc T. Ossoway, second year | Forestry Club · feeding rota, week four · mortified
WANT: The log to have stopped at week three. It did not, and he wrote the rest himself.
VOICE: Fast, over-explaining, correcting himself mid-sentence, going redder.
BREAK: The rabbits ran out in week three and he did not stop feeding it in week four.
LINE: It was still small in April. You have to understand it was *still small in April*.
USE: He knows the way in, the heat trick, and roughly how long the crop takes.
:::

:::puzzle Six Feet of Crop | the trunk · four feet down, and counting
LOOKS: A swollen length of trunk, warm to the touch, with a ribbon showing through the wall at about four feet.
SOLVE: Heat, salt, or borrowed acid on the crop wall. Any one works. Cutting works too, and costs whoever cuts.
FAIL: Round eleven, and it shuts. He is not dead -- he is *unavailable until April*, which the Club considers worse.
:::
:::

:::item The Mascot's Ribbon | Cloth, university colours -- the crop, four feet down
Fourteen years of an elderly boar being walked across a lawn in front of two hundred first-years. Returned with him, the Club's silence holds. Returned without him, it does not, and the Club is nine students who have all just seen the inside of a crop.
:::

If it's going fast: a second drag channel, older and wider, with a collar at the end of it.
Cliffhanger: the pond on the Botany lawn ripples at four in the morning, from underneath.

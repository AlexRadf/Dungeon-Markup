:::read ON THE BOARD -- AS POSTED
The Club's specimen in greenhouse three has outgrown its bed. This morning it took the university mascot -- the live one, the one with the ribbon -- off the front lawn and through the koi pond. No water was displaced.

We would like this handled quietly and inside the Club. He is fourteen years old and cannot be replaced.
:::

:::cols 2
:::box plain | ON THE WAY THERE
Greenhouse three has been the Club's since Botany stopped inspecting it. Feeding is logged by a second year and the log stops mid-page.

The pond has been refilled twice this year and Estates cannot find the leak. The mascot is a boar: elderly, badly behaved, beloved. Two students have reported the reflection in the pond not matching the sky. One of them has since dropped out. The Club has told nobody.
:::

Growth this week: **eleven feet.** Signed: T. Ossoway, second year.

:::box plain | HANDOUT -- PEN & INK -- ANATOMICAL PLATE
A cross-section from a botanical monograph: the crop of a titan-class carnivorous tree, cut lengthways, six feet of it, with a ruled depth scale down the left margin. Fine parallel hatching for the fibrous walls, stipple for the fluid, lettered and captioned in the flat voice of a reference work. The horror is entirely in the layers, and they must be drawn as calmly as everything else: bone at the bottom, a collar and a length of ribbon at four feet, and at the top -- undissolved, whole, still in the posture of walking -- something recently alive. Caption: *Digestion is not fast. It is only certain.*
:::

:::secrets
1. The Titan cannot be killed in ten rounds by anything the party is carrying. Not with a good plan. Not at all.
2. It can be made to open -- heat, salt, or enough acid borrowed from its own hydras.
3. He is alive at round ten and not at round eleven. That is the entire clock and it should be counted aloud.
4. Ossoway fed it rabbits, then stopped writing down what else he fed it.
5. The pond has belonged to the plant and not to the university for about a year.
6. The whole jungle is one plant. The hydras are fed by it, the canopy is it, the drag is where it moved.
7. The way home shuts when the canopy shuts, and the canopy is shutting from the moment they arrive.
8. The student who dropped out did not drop out. Check the second layer of the plate.
:::

:::read WHERE IT STARTS -- READ IT AT THE POND, AND START COUNTING
The reflection in the koi pond is not the sky. You go in dry and you come out dry on the other side, into heat, under a closed canopy, and the whole jungle is one plant. You can still hear him. He sounds furious. He sounds a long way in.
:::

:::beats
1. **Through the pond.** Dry in, dry out, into heat under a closed canopy. You can still hear him, and the canopy has already started shutting behind you.
2. **Rounds one to three: the drag.** A channel torn through the moss, going inward, easy to follow. That is the trap and it is also the answer.
3. **Round four: the beds.** Hydras both sides and the only path runs between them. Going round costs three rounds you do not have.
4. **Round five: the ribbon** comes free in the crop and floats up the fluid where everyone can see it.
5. **Rounds six to nine.** Heat, salt, or acid borrowed from the beds, applied to six feet of trunk, by somebody with a free hand and somebody else buying them the time.
6. **Round ten: he stops.** Not dramatically. He simply stops, and the table has been counting.
7. **Round eleven.** The canopy closes over the pond, and the pond is on the Botany lawn.
:::

:::cols 2
:::place The Pond-Side | the way home, and it is shutting
- You arrive dry, which is the first thing anybody notices and the worst.
- Heat like a glasshouse in August, and a canopy closed overhead in every direction.
- Behind you the moss is already knitting across the way you came.
:::

:::place The Drag | a channel torn inward through the moss
- Six feet wide and going one way, and something heavy went along it this morning.
- Acid scarring along both edges, older than the drag itself.
- It runs dead straight between the hydra beds, because whatever made it does not care.
:::

:::place The Hydra Beds | acid, and the only path runs between them
- Three heads each, and the stumps close over and regrow while you watch.
- The moss between the beds is burned to the stone and still steaming.
- They are fed by the canopy. They cannot leave and they will not stop.
:::

:::place The Crop | six feet of swollen trunk at the base of the tree
- Warm to the hand, and moving, slowly, on a count of about nine.
- A ribbon visible through the wall at four feet, university colours.
- Bone at the bottom. A collar at four feet. Something whole at the top.
:::

:::place The Canopy | the roof, the walls, and the way home
- Closed over completely, and closing further, one round at a time, since you arrived.
- It feeds the hydras, it grew the drag, and it owns the pond. It is all one plant.
- When it shuts over the water, the water is still on the Botany lawn.
:::

:::roll d6 | COMPLICATION -- ONCE EVERY THREE ROUNDS

3 The moss gives. DC 13 DEX or waist-deep.
4 He goes quiet for one round, then starts again.
5 Acid underfoot. 1d6, and your boots after that.
6 A second drag channel, older, crossing yours.

:::cols 2
:::box red | TROUBLE
- Hydras spit acid and regrow the head you cut, because the canopy is growing it back for them.
- The canopy closing behind you, one round at a time, from the moment you arrive.
- Ten rounds, and then no mascot. Count them out loud at the table.
- The Titan does not have to move to win. It only has to not open.
:::

:::box gold | WAYS OUT
- Cut him out and run, which costs somebody an arm in the crop and is still the fastest way.
- Make it spit -- heat, salt, or its own hydras' acid, delivered to the right six feet.
- Seal the pond and lose him, which the Club will accept and never forgive.
:::

:::stat The Titan | CR 8 | AC 17 | HP 260 | SPD 0 ft | STR +7 | DEX -3 | CON +6 | INT -4 | WIS +2 | CHA -2

**Two vines** +9, 30 ft: 16 (3d8+7) bludgeoning and grappled (escape DC 17). It drops you in the crop at 20 ft of movement.

SOLVE: Heat, salt, or borrowed acid on the crop wall. Any one works. Cutting works too and costs whoever cuts.
FAIL: Round eleven. He is not dead of the plant; he is dead of the ten rounds, and everyone at the table counted them.

Fourteen years of an elderly boar being walked across a lawn in front of two hundred first-years. Returned with him, the Club's silence holds. Returned without him, it does not, and the Club is nine students who have all just seen the inside of a crop.

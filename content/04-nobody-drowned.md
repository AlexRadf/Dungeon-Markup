:::pitch
A tally-clerk drowned on the third floor of a dry house, in a dry room, in a dry season. Nothing in this city admits to being wet.
!! THEME -- the water has been rising for eleven years and everyone agreed not to mention it.
:::

:::web THE WEB -- WHAT LEADS WHERE
No order. Every node has three clues pointing at it, so they can miss two and still arrive.
:::

:::cols 2
:::node A | The Dry Room | place | start
Latched from inside. Vole face down in a coin-sized puddle, lungs full.
-> B  It is lock water, not canal water. Sweet, and nobody but the bailiff turns a lock.
-> C  Salt crust on the window latch. One place in this city still blesses salt.
-> D  A chandler's tally-stick under the bed, notched for deliveries nobody made.
:::

:::node B | Marta Cull | person
Thirty years on the locks. Knows exactly what she is paid not to see.
-> C  Her log has the wharf chapel drawing lock water at night, four months running.
-> D  The Guild of Wicks pays her weekly. She resents the amount, not the arrangement.
-> E  She has the survey of the old cistern, and will trade it for a safe bed.
:::

:::node C | The Salt Chapel | place
Deconsecrated eleven years ago. Still swept, still lit.
-> B  A ledger of lock openings in the bailiff's hand, in a year she swears she never wrote.
-> D  Guild-stamped wax. Nobody else in the city is allowed to make it.
-> E  Wet footprints, going down and never up, from an altar with nothing behind it.
:::

:::node D | The Guild of Wicks | group
Respectable. Their cellar has flooded nightly for a year and nobody asks why.
-> B  A bribe booked weekly to a *harbour officer*. One name, one address, no discretion at all.
-> C  Six months of wax delivered to a chapel that has been shut since the flood.
-> E  Their cellar floods at the same hour the cistern fills, and the water tastes of chapel salt.
:::

:::node E | The Cistern | place
Forty feet down, unmapped since the flood. Something in it has kept eleven years of water polite.
:::
:::

:::cols 2
:::reveal Vole was drowned somewhere else and carried home
- A: The room is dry and the man is not. Water does not tidy up after itself.
- B: Lock water is sweet. The canal is not, and neither is the rain.
- D: His tally-stick is notched for deliveries the Guild never made — he was owed a meeting.
:::

:::reveal The Guild has been feeding the thing in the cistern
- B: Marta's log: lock water drawn at night, four months, on the Guild's account.
- C: Guild wax burning in a chapel that has no congregation left to burn it for.
- D: The cellar floods on a schedule, and a schedule is somebody's decision.
:::
:::

:::valve
If they stall: the cistern comes up through a street drain, once, and goes back down.
:::

## The rooms

:::room 1 | The registry stair
The door sticks shut. DC 12 Athletics, or press on and come back for it.
:::

:::room 2 | Collapsed reading room
**Homework Swarm x2** rises the moment anyone reads aloud. The shelves are still alphabetical.
:::

:::room 3 | The flooded run
**Gutterwick** lives in the pipe. Difficult terrain, waist deep, and something down there is counting.
:::

:::room 4 | The sealed office
**THE CLUE.** Four desks, eleven years of dust, and the Somnolent Stamp.
:::

+++

## What is down there

:::stat Homework Swarm | CR 1/2 | AC 12 | HP 22 | SPD fly 30 ft
**Papercuts** +4, 5 ft, all in its space: 10 (4d4) slashing, or 5 (2d4) at half HP.
**Swarm.** Resists bludgeoning, piercing and slashing. Vulnerable to fire.
**Recite (1/day).** Reads a dead student's essay in a PC's own voice: DC 11 WIS or frightened.
> Area 2 · x2 · rises the moment anyone reads aloud
:::

:::stat Gutterwick | CR 1 | AC 13 | HP 27 | SPD 20 ft, swim 40 ft
**Grasping pull** +5, 10 ft: 6 (1d8+2) bludgeoning and the target is dragged 10 ft into the water.
**Toll.** Will not attack anyone who gives it something written. It cannot read.
> Area 3 · the pipe · retreats if the water is drained
:::

:::roll 1d6 | What the party hears in the dark
1 Water, moving the wrong way.
2 A bell, two floors up, rung once.
3 Their own footsteps, delayed.
4 Somebody filing paper.
5 Nothing. Genuinely nothing, for a full minute.
6 A voice reading a name off a list. It is not one of theirs.
:::

:::box accent | WORTH TAKING
**The Somnolent Stamp** (area 4) -- the single most important object in the campaign.
:::

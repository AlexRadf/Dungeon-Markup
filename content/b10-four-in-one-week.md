:::pitch
Four students have gone to marble in one corridor since Monday, all four facing the same way, and all four still very much present about it. Campus Police have jurisdiction over property and nothing whatsoever else, as Detective Miller will tell you twice. He would like a cause by morning.
!! THEME -- nobody is dead, everybody is furious, and the woman responsible is further gone to stone than any of them.
:::

:::read WHERE IT STARTS -- READ IT IN THE CORRIDOR, AT NIGHT, WITH ONE TORCH
Four statues in a corridor, all mid-step, all facing the same way. The mirror at the end has a crack running corner to corner that does not go through the glass. Miller wants a cause by morning and has issued you one torch and no authority of any kind.
:::

:::web THE WEB -- FIVE PLACES, NO ORDER
The mirror opens on three clues, an hour each, and you feel every hour go past.
:::

:::cols 2
:::node A | The Corridor | place | start
Four of them, mid-step. Which way they were walking is the first real clue in the building.
-> B  All four sat the same midterm, and three still have the paper in their rooms.
-> C  All four face the same way, and the way is the bathroom, and the mirror is in the bathroom.
-> D  The floor is wet. Not damp -- wet, and it tastes faintly of lens polish.
:::

:::node B | The Exam Hall | place
The midterm they all sat, and the paper itself, which nobody has thought to read.
-> C  Her working is in question six. Read it as a diagram and it is the crack in the mirror.
-> D  The lens order's account number belongs to a real department, and that department has a basement.
-> E  One person set this paper, and the four who went to marble are the four who answered question six.
:::

:::node C | The Mirror | place
Three clues open it, an hour each, and the hours are the fifth student.
-> B  The crack does not go through the glass. It goes through what is behind it, and it is a diagram.
-> D  Open it and there is wet stone, lens benches, and rows of people standing very still and looking bored.
-> E  Something looks back, wearing goggles, and it looks away first.
:::

:::node D | The Lab Beyond | place
Wet stone, lens benches, and six half-stone undergraduates standing in rows.
-> B  They are in field jackets holding exam papers, and one of them is marked.
-> C  The lenses on the bench are ground to a reversing curve. So is the crack.
-> E  She is stone to the elbow, has been for a year, and the fourth pair was declined.
:::

:::node E | The Reader | person
Faculty. Blind behind the goggles on purpose, running out of glass, and out of ideas.
-> D  She has one pair left and will not use it on herself. That is the whole of her.
:::
:::

+++

:::secrets
1. Nobody is dead. All four are reversible, all four can hear you, and all four will have opinions afterwards.
2. It has run the wrong way for a year. It started with her, and she has been trying to stop it ever since.
3. The four sat the midterm she set, and her working is in question six for anybody who reads it as a diagram.
4. Reversal exists. It runs backwards through the same lenses, and she has exactly one pair left.
5. Take the goggles and she is blind, stone to the elbow, and enormously relieved to be asked.
6. Toller Vance was found *inside* the bathroom facing the mirror. The other three were found facing him.
:::

:::cols 2
:::box red | TROUBLE
- The fifth student, tonight, while you are still asking questions in the corridor.
- The goggles: she sees you first, in every room, because that is what they are for.
- The statues degrade. Detail first, then the face, and reversal cannot put back what has gone.
:::

:::box gold | WAYS OUT
- Take the goggles. Blind, stone to the elbow, and she will talk for as long as you let her.
- Fetch the fourth pair from Highport, which the grinder declined to make and can be talked round on.
- Hand Miller the account number and let Finance finish it, in eleven weeks and four more students.
:::
:::

:::cols 2
:::npc Det. Miller, Campus Police | Twenty years of property crime · tired, decent, powerless
WANT: A cause by morning, in writing, from somebody whose jurisdiction he need not explain.
VOICE: Flat. Repeats the important sentence twice so you cannot claim you missed it.
BREAK: Forty readings of the incident sheet, and he knows number four faced the wrong way.
LINE: I have jurisdiction over property. I have jurisdiction over property.
:::

:::puzzle Question Six | the exam hall · read it as a diagram, not a question
LOOKS: A midterm question on refraction with a worked figure under it. Printed. Forty copies.
SOLVE: The figure is a lens ground to a reversing curve, the same shape as the crack. Say so aloud and the mirror opens.
FAIL: Never a dead end -- but each wrong clue is an hour, and the hours are the fifth student.
:::

:::item The Fourth Pair | Wondrous, rare -- Highport, declined
Basilisk-grade, reversing curve: the wearer cannot see out, and the sight goes in. Worn, they run petrification backwards through anything already stone. The grinder declined the order in writing and can be talked round, for a price that is not money.

:::pitch
Four students have gone to marble in one corridor since Monday, all four facing the same way. Campus Police have jurisdiction over property and nothing else, and Detective Miller will tell you so twice. He wants a cause by morning.
!! THEME -- the statues are still students, and every hour you spend asking is an hour they spend degrading.
:::

:::read ON THE BOARD -- AS POSTED
Four students in Dorm 4 have gone to marble since Monday. All four sat the same midterm. All four were found in the bathroom corridor, facing the same way.

We have no jurisdiction over whatever the fifth one is going to be. Establish the cause before morning. Do not break the statues. They are still students.
:::

:::cols 2
:::box plain | ON THE WAY THERE
Dorm 4 is the old block, due for refurbishment, still full. All four were second years on the same module.

The bathroom mirror has been cracked since before the first one and Estates have a work order open on it. Campus Police have jurisdiction over property and nothing else, which Miller will say twice. The families have not been told. Three students have already moved out and one of them will not say why.
:::

:::box dark | HANDOUT -- INCIDENT SHEET, CAMPUS POLICE, COPIED TO FINANCE FOR DAMAGES
**1.** Ilma Reck, found 0400, corridor, facing the bathroom. **2.** J. Ambry, found 0350, same. **3.** Name withheld at family request, found 0410, same. **4.** Toller Vance, found 0345, **INSIDE** the bathroom, facing the mirror.

Damage to fittings: none. Damage to mirror: pre-existing crack, work order 4471 refers.
:::
:::

:::box plain | HANDOUT -- PEN & INK -- TRADE CARD
A lens grinder's work order from Highport, printed on a trade card: engraved header showing a lens in section with its curve marked, a ruled scale of focal lengths, the firm's name in an ornamented face. Precise, commercial, entirely unmysterious. The order beneath, in the customer's hand: *Two, matched. Basilisk-grade. Reversing curve -- the wearer must not see out; the sight must go in.* Signed with a department account number and no name. Along the bottom, added later in a different pen: *Third pair to this account this year. Declined the fourth.*
:::

:::secrets
1. She is faculty. The account number on the lens order belongs to a real department and always has.
2. The petrification is running the wrong way and has been for a year. It started with her.
3. The four sat the midterm she set. Her working is in the paper, in question six, for anyone who reads it as a diagram.
4. Reversal exists. It runs backwards through the same lenses and she has one pair left.
5. Take the goggles and she is blind, stone to the elbow, and entirely willing to talk.
6. Toller Vance was found inside the bathroom facing the mirror. The other three were found facing him.
7. The crack in the mirror does not go through the glass. It goes through what is behind it.
8. The thralls in the lab are students. Three of them moved out of Dorm 4 in the last fortnight.
:::

:::cols 2
:::box red | TROUBLE
- The fifth student, tonight, while you are still asking questions in the corridor.
- The goggles: she sees you first, every time, in every room, because that is what they are for.
- The thralls are students. Every one of them was somebody's roommate a fortnight ago.
- The statues are degrading. Detail goes first, then the face, and reversal cannot put back what has gone.
:::

:::box gold | WAYS OUT
- Take the goggles. Blind, stone to the elbow, and she will talk for as long as you let her.
- Fetch her the fourth pair from Highport, which the grinder declined to make and can be persuaded to.
- Give Miller the account number and let Finance finish it, which takes eleven weeks and four more students.
:::

:::cols 2
:::npc Det. Miller, Campus Police | Twenty years of property crime · tired, decent, powerless
WANT: A cause by morning, in writing, from somebody whose jurisdiction he does not have to explain.
VOICE: Flat. Repeats the important sentence twice, deliberately, so you cannot claim not to have heard it.
BREAK: He has read the incident sheet forty times and knows number four was facing the wrong way.
LINE: I have jurisdiction over property. I have jurisdiction over property.
USE: He can seal a corridor, open a work order, and get you into the exam hall. Nothing else.
:::

:::puzzle Question Six | the exam hall · read it as a diagram, not a question
LOOKS: A midterm question about refraction with a worked figure beneath it, printed, forty copies.
SOLVE: The figure is a lens ground to a reversing curve, and it is the same shape as the crack in the mirror. Anyone who has seen both says so out loud and the mirror opens.
FAIL: Never a dead end -- but each wrong clue is an hour, and the hours are the fifth student.
:::

:::item The Fourth Pair | Wondrous, rare -- Highport, declined
Two matched lenses, basilisk-grade, reversing curve: the wearer cannot see out and the sight goes in. Worn, they run the petrification backwards through anything already stone. The grinder declined the order in writing and can be talked round for a price that is not money.
:::

:::pitch
The university has held the perpetual care contract on Kharn's crypt for two hundred years and has performed roughly none of it. Since January the crypt has started paying anyway. Freshly struck silver, coming out through the offering slot, every week, on time. Mortuary Studies would very much like to know who it is in business with.
!! THEME -- nobody down there is haunting anybody. He is two hundred years in credit and he would like somebody to come and do the gutters.
:::

:::read WHERE IT STARTS -- READ IT AT THE GATE, IN DAYLIGHT
Two pillars, a lintel, a sealed door, and a heap of silver under the slot that nobody local will go near. Pick a coin up and it is warm, the way a coin is warm out of somebody's hand. The gate has not opened in two hundred years, and somebody has oiled the hinges.
:::

Sealed two hundred years and maintained every day of them. **No light, and no dust either.** Everything down here is swept, oiled and in good repair, which is comfortably the most alarming fact on this page.

:::rooms | AREA | WHAT IS THERE
1 | The gate | Sealed, oiled, and the slot goes both ways. Warm silver underneath, more of it every week. It opens **outward**.
2 | The counting room | Kharn's ledgers, still kept, in ink that has not dried. Bullion in and coin out on one page. The arrears column ends in a figure with a date beside it, and the date is soon.
3 | The mint | Press, dies, blanks, and finished silver too warm to bag. Nothing here is turning the press. It is turned from below, on a beat.
4 | The cell at the bottom | **Behind the secret door**, which is not locked and never has been. Kharn is in here, counting, and has been since January.
:::

:::secrets
1. Kharn is paying for care the university has not performed since 1189. He does not know that. He is assuming good faith, which is the sad part.
2. He is counting the arrears. When the sum comes right he intends to come up and collect the *service* -- not the money.
3. The silver is struck below, from bullion nobody has ever carried in, by a press turned on a beat.
4. Take a coin and you become the university's agent, and the work becomes yours. The ledger records the transfer within the hour, correctly.
5. Divinity's man came in spring, took a handful, and walked out whistling. He is in Oakhaven and he is still very much on the books.
6. The gate opens outward, from the inside, and always did. The hinges have been oiled by somebody who intends to use them.
:::

+++

:::cols 2
:::box red | TROUBLE
- Nothing is decayed and everything is maintained. This place is not ruined, it is *staffed*.
- Take their coin and you are on the books, named correctly, within the hour.
- Being on the books means the outstanding work is yours: a hundred and thirty-six years of it.
- Whatever does the maintenance is doing it right now, in the next room, and does not stop for anybody.
:::

:::box gold | WAYS OUT
- Do the maintenance. Badly. Two hundred years of arrears cleared in one honest afternoon with a broom.
- Take nothing at all, break the seal, and let Mortuary Studies argue with an empty crypt.
- Pay him in his own coin, and let Finance explain to the Chancellor where it came from.
:::
:::

:::beats
1. **Payments keep rising.** He is compounding, and he is doing it correctly.
2. **The arrears settle in full**, and an account in credit is an account owed a service.
3. **The gate opens outward from the inside**, and Kharn walks to Oakhaven to be attended to, with the paperwork in order, in his hand.
:::

:::cols 2
:::stat Kharn, Warden of Arrears | CR 8 | AC 17 | HP 152 | SPD 30 ft | STR +4 | DEX +1 | CON +5 | INT +4 | WIS +5 | CHA +6
**Ledger touch** +8, 5 ft: 18 (4d6+4) necrotic, and the target's name is entered. Entered creatures cannot leave while the account stands.
**Called to account.** One creature within 60 ft: DC 16 CHA or paralysed while he reads out what it has taken. Ends if it puts the item down.
SAVES: WIS +9, CHA +10 · **Immune** poison, exhaustion, frightened
**He will not strike first.** He states the sum, waits, states it again, and fights only when the account is refused.
> The cell · one · the fight is the failure state and he will say so first
:::

:::stat Attendant of the Contract | CR 3 | AC 15 | HP 45 | SPD 30 ft | STR +3 | DEX +2 | CON +3 | INT -2 | WIS +1 | CHA -3
**Two tools** +5, 5 ft: 8 (1d10+3) bludgeoning each. It is holding a broom and it uses it like one.
**On shift.** It will not touch anybody who is working. Sweep, oil, polish or carry and it walks straight past you.
**Never stops.** At 0 hp it puts its tools down neatly first.
> Stair, counting room, mint · x4 · this is what maintenance looks like when somebody actually does it
:::

:::cols 2
:::puzzle The Perpetual Care Contract | the counting room · an afternoon's work, or a fight
LOOKS: A hundred and thirty-six years of undone maintenance, itemised, in a hand that has never once been wrong.
SOLVE: Do some of it. Sweep, oil, re-letter a stone, log it, sign it. The contract sets no standard and Kharn is not a pedant.
FAIL: Sign it as yourselves and the contract transfers. In perpetuity. You will be told this clearly and you will do it anyway.
:::

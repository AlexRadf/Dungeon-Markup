:::pitch
The accreditation inspector arrives at dawn, walks every corridor and opens every door. There are eleven things in this university that must not be behind an open door. You have from dawn until his lunch, and then you have his lunch, which is one hour.
!! THEME -- eleven items, one trolley, and a list that is one item longer than it says it is.
:::

:::read ON THE BOARD -- AS POSTED
The inspector arrives at dawn to renew our accreditation. He will walk every corridor. He will open every door. He is incorruptible -- the last man in the post closed a college at Highport and slept well afterwards.

There are eleven things in this university that must not be behind an open door. You have from dawn until his lunch. Then you have his lunch, which is one hour.
:::

:::cols 2
:::box plain | ON THE WAY THERE
The inspector takes lunch at noon, precisely, for one hour -- the only unwatched window in the day. The Proctor's list runs to eleven items and he will name nine of them aloud.

Departments loathe each other and will not co-operate without leverage. Astronomy keeps something on the roof under a tarp. Pen 9 in the animal facility has been locked from the inside since spring. One item on that list is not an object.
:::

:::box dark | HANDOUT -- THE INSPECTOR'S ITINERARY, AS CIRCULATED
**06:00** Arrival, west gate. Reception by the Chancellor. · **07:00** Libraries and reading rooms. · **08:30** Divinity, including the chapel and its fittings. · **10:00** Animal facility and outbuildings. · **11:00** Astronomy, *including the roof.* · **12:00 LUNCH -- Chancellor's lodging -- one hour** · **13:00** The cellars. · **14:30** Thaumaturgy, the old kiln. · **16:00** Private interview with the Proctor. · **17:00** Determination.

*Footnote, printed small:* The inspector will not be accompanied at any point by a member of the department under inspection. **The inspector does not eat during lunch.**
:::
:::

:::box plain | HANDOUT -- THE PROCTOR'S LIST, HALF A SHEET, CARRIED IN A POCKET SOME TIME
**1.** The bell, Divinity. *Not to be rung, not to be seen, not to be mentioned.* · **2.** Pen 9, animal facility. *Say it's a store room. It is a store room.* · **3.** The item under the tarp, Astronomy roof. · **4.** ███████████████████████ · **5.** Cellar stair, dining hall. *Wall it. Today. I don't care what's behind it.* · **6.** The Hessle bequest, third gallery. · **7.** ███████████████████████ · **8.** Whatever Thaumaturgy is doing in the old kiln. · **9.** The tenth item on this list. · **10.** ███████████████████████ · **11.** The Proctor.

*The blacking-out is heavy, done with a nib, gone over several times. Item 9 is not redacted and is worse for it.*
:::

:::secrets
1. The list is one item longer than it says. Item 9 is the tenth, and the tenth is redacted.
2. Item 11 is not a joke. The Proctor has been here longer than the building has.
3. Pen 9 is locked from the inside by something that will co-operate if it is asked properly.
4. Astronomy's tarp covers something taken from the last inspection, thirty years ago, and never given back.
5. The inspector does not eat. He spends the noon hour walking the corridors alone, which is when he finds things.
6. Item 5 is the cellar stair, and walling it is job 12, and the Proctor knows exactly what he is asking for.
7. Departments will trade. Divinity wants the kiln put out; Thaumaturgy wants Pen 9 emptied; Astronomy wants its fitting kept.
8. The last man in this post closed a college at Highport and slept well afterwards. He will say so, once, kindly.
:::

:::read WHERE IT STARTS -- READ IT AT QUARTER TO SIX, IN THE DARK
The side door of the Proctor's lodge. He hands over a folded list, tells you nine of the eleven out loud, and does not tell you the other two. Then he says good luck in a voice that has done this before. The inspector's car is already at the west gate. It is fifteen minutes early.
:::

:::cols 2
:::box red | TROUBLE
- Eleven items, one trolley, and every corridor between them is a corridor he might be in.
- Departments will not co-operate without leverage, and the only leverage available is each other.
- Item 9, which is the tenth item, which is redacted, which means somebody has to open the list.
- The hour at noon, which is not free, because he does not eat and he does walk.
:::

:::box gold | WAYS OUT
- Hide all eleven and pass clean, which nobody has managed since the Proctor was appointed.
- Hand him item 11 and pass. The Proctor has been expecting this for a very long time.
- Fail deliberately, which three departments would pay considerably more than eighty-five gold for.
:::

:::stat The Occupant of Pen 9 | CR 4 | AC 16 | HP 85 | SPD 40 ft | STR +4 | DEX +3 | CON +4 | INT +2 | WIS +3 | CHA +1
**It locked the door from the inside.** It has hands, it has reasons, and it has been in there since spring.
**Two strikes** +7, 5 ft: 11 (2d6+4) slashing each, and only ever in the last round of a conversation that has failed.
**Asked properly.** It understands Eldorian, it will not speak it, and it will co-operate for one specific thing.
**Will not be moved.** Immune to charm and to being pushed, pulled or teleported out of that pen.
> Pen 9 · one · every party that fights this has skipped the conversation, and it is a short conversation

:::npc The Proctor | Office of the Proctor · item 11 on his own list
WANT: The accreditation renewed and the list unread. In that order, and he means the order.
VOICE: Dry, exact, unhurried. Tells you nine things and lets the silence do the other two.
BREAK: He wrote item 11 himself, in his own hand, and he has carried the sheet in his pocket for some time.
LINE: There are eleven. I have told you nine. You will manage.
USE: He will not explain the redactions and he will answer any direct question about himself honestly.
:::
:::

:::cols 2
:::puzzle Item Nine | anywhere · a list that is longer than it says
LOOKS: Eleven numbered lines, three of them blacked out with a nib, and line 9 reading *the tenth item on this list.*
SOLVE: Hold it to a window. The nib went over the redactions several times and the paper remembers the strokes. Item 10 can be read in about a minute by anyone patient.
FAIL: Never a dead end. Not reading it means arriving at the tenth item at noon, with the inspector already in the corridor.
:::

:::pitch
The accreditation inspector arrives at dawn, walks every corridor and opens every door. There are eleven things in this university that must not be behind an open door when he does. You have from dawn until his lunch, and then you have his lunch, which is one hour.
!! THEME -- eleven items, one trolley, and a list that is quietly one item longer than it says it is.
:::

:::read WHERE IT STARTS -- READ IT AT QUARTER TO SIX, IN THE DARK
The side door of the Proctor's lodge. He hands over a folded half-sheet, tells you nine of the eleven out loud, and does not tell you the other two. Then he wishes you luck in the voice of a man who has done this before. The inspector's car is already at the west gate. It is fifteen minutes early.
:::

:::beats
1. **05:45, the side door.** Nine items said aloud and two that are not. The list is in your hand and it is half a sheet of paper.
2. **06:00, he arrives fifteen minutes early**, and the Chancellor has to fill the time with something, and does.
3. **08:30, Divinity.** The chapel, the fittings, and one cupboard with no lock on it.
4. **10:00, the animal facility.** Pen 9 is still locked from the inside and the agreed story is that it is a store room.
5. **11:00, Astronomy**, *including the roof*, which is on the itinerary in italics because somebody insisted on it.
6. **12:00, the unwatched hour** -- which is not unwatched, because he does not eat, and he does walk. Then 16:00, the interview, and 17:00, determination.
:::

:::cols 2
:::place Pen 9 | locked from the inside since spring
- The lock is on the inside of the door, and it was fitted from the inside too.
- Straw changed weekly. Somebody is feeding it and nobody has signed for the feed.
- It understands you perfectly. It will not answer in Eldorian, and it will co-operate for exactly one thing.
:::

:::place The Astronomy Roof | under a tarp, guyed down, not discussed
- Kiln-fired ceramic anchors at all four corners, and exactly one kiln on this campus is lit.
- Whatever is under it has Divinity's mark cut into the base.
- It came off this roof in a crate at the last inspection, thirty years ago, and went straight to Pen 9.
:::

:::place The Proctor's Lodge | where it starts, and item 11
- A side door, a folded half-sheet, and nine items said out loud in the dark.
- Three lines blacked out with a nib, gone over several times, still readable at a window.
- Item 11 is the Proctor. He wrote it himself and he has carried the sheet a while.
:::
:::

:::secrets
1. The list is one item longer than it says. Item 9 is the tenth, and the tenth is redacted.
2. Item 11 is not a joke. The Proctor has been here rather longer than the building has.
3. Pen 9 is locked from the inside by something that will co-operate at once if it is asked properly.
4. The inspector does not eat. He spends the noon hour walking the corridors alone, which is when he finds things.
5. Departments will trade. Divinity wants the kiln put out, Thaumaturgy wants Pen 9 emptied, Astronomy wants its fitting kept.
6. Astronomy's tarp covers something taken at the last inspection, thirty years ago, and never given back.
:::

+++

:::cols 2
:::box red | TROUBLE
- Eleven items, one trolley, and every corridor between them is a corridor he might be in.
- Departments will not co-operate without leverage, and the only leverage available is each other.
- Item 9, which is the tenth item, which is redacted, which means somebody has to open the list.
- The hour at noon, which is not free, because he does not eat and he does walk.
:::

:::box gold | WAYS OUT
- Hide all eleven and pass clean, which nobody has managed since the Proctor was appointed.
- Hand him item 11 and pass. The Proctor has been expecting this for a very long time.
- Fail on purpose, which three departments would pay considerably more than eighty-five gold for.
:::
:::

:::beats
1. **08:30**, the chapel, and the cupboard with no lock on it.
2. **12:00**, he leaves the Chancellor's lodging by the far door, alone, and starts walking.
3. **16:00**, the private interview with the Proctor, door shut. **17:00**, determination.
:::

:::cols 2
:::stat The Occupant of Pen 9 | CR 4 | AC 16 | HP 85 | SPD 40 ft | STR +4 | DEX +3 | CON +4 | INT +2 | WIS +3 | CHA +1
**It locked the door from the inside.** It has hands, it has reasons, and it has been in there since spring.
**Two strikes** +7, 5 ft: 11 (2d6+4) slashing each, and only ever in the last round of a conversation that has failed.
**Asked properly.** It understands Eldorian, it will not speak it, and it will co-operate for one specific thing.
**Will not be moved.** Immune to charm and to being pushed, pulled or teleported out of that pen.
> Pen 9 · one · everyone who fights this skipped the conversation, and it is a short conversation
:::

:::npc The Proctor | Office of the Proctor · item 11 on his own list
WANT: The accreditation renewed and the list unread. In that order, and he means the order.
VOICE: Dry, exact, unhurried. Tells you nine things and lets the silence handle the other two.
BREAK: He wrote item 11 himself, in his own hand, and has carried the sheet in his pocket some time.
LINE: There are eleven. I have told you nine. You will manage.
:::
:::

:::cols 2
:::puzzle Item Nine | anywhere · a list that is longer than it says
LOOKS: Eleven numbered lines, three blacked out with a nib, and line 9 reading *the tenth item on this list.*
SOLVE: Hold it to a window. The nib went over the redactions several times and the paper remembers the strokes. A patient reader has item 10 in about a minute.
FAIL: Never a dead end. Not reading it means meeting the tenth item at noon, with the inspector already in the corridor.
:::

:::pitch
Aumreth, who ended things kindly, fell a hundred years ago and has been dying ever since. A god dies from the outside in. Somewhere near the middle, it is still a hundred years from finished — and someone has gone in to hurry it along.
!! THEME -- mercy, administered by people with no authority to administer it.
:::

:::read STRONG START -- OPEN HERE, EVERY TIME
The wound in the shoulder is four hundred feet of daylight and cold. You are eighty feet inside it when the corpse takes a breath — the first in nine days, the surveyors said — and the passage narrows around you to the width of two people, and holds, and does not let go for a count of six. Then it opens. Somewhere ahead, in the dark, something enormous finishes a note it started before you arrived.
:::

:::cols 2
:::scenes POTENTIAL SCENES -- NOT A SEQUENCE
- The Coroner's camp, still warm, wards still up, nobody in it.
- The breath. Every 9 minutes, everything narrows. Roll initiative or don't.
- The Choir finds them, and sings a name that belongs to a PC.
- An Antibody arrives and treats the party as an infection, politely.
- A survivor of the first expedition, alive because the god is keeping them.
- The heart chamber, and the choice about the spike.
- Getting out, against the breath, with the thing they took.
:::

## The cast

:::npc Vess Ordeal | Human necrotheologist · calm, exhausted, correct
WANT: Aumreth to finish. A god dying slowly poisons the eleven towns downhill.
VOICE: Flat, precise, kind. Explains everything. Never raises it.
BREAK: She is right, and knows the party will not forgive her for it.
LINE: I am not killing it. I am closing its eyes.
USE: Not a boss. A person with a better argument than you expected.
:::

:::npc Kell of the First Expedition | Dwarf surveyor · ninety years in, not aged a day
WANT: To leave. Has wanted this for ninety years.
VOICE: Whispers. Stops mid-sentence to listen for the breath.
BREAK: The god is keeping him alive. Leaving kills him, and he knows.
LINE: It isn't cruel. It just doesn't know how to let go of anything.
:::
:::

:::secrets
1. The breath is not breathing. It is a heartbeat, slowed by a factor of nine hundred.
2. Vess Ordeal is here to kill the god, and has said so, in writing, to three governments.
3. Eleven towns downhill are dying of what leaks out. Their water runs warm and forgives.
4. The Choir sings the names of everyone Aumreth ever eased. It is still learning new ones.
5. It knows a PC's name. It has always known it. Ask that player when they nearly died.
6. Kell has been inside ninety years. He has not eaten. He is not hungry. He is not fine.
7. The Antibodies are not guards. They are grief, given a shape and a job.
8. The spike in the heart is Vess's, driven a third of the way, and it is working.
9. Pulling the spike out saves the god and dooms the towns. Both. Not one.
10. Something outside is waiting for the god to finish, and it is patient about it.
:::

:::valve
If it's dragging: The breath comes early. Everything narrows, and something is in here with them.
If it's going fast: Kell is following them, and has been for an hour.
Cliffhanger: They surface. The wound has closed behind them by a foot.
:::

### What is down there

:::cols 2
:::stat Marrow Choir | CR 13 | AC 18 | HP 195 | SPD fly 40 ft | STR +0 | DEX +5 | CON +4 | INT -2 | WIS +6 | CHA +7
**Chorus** +10, 60 ft, three targets: 27 (6d8) psychic, and it learns the target's name.
**Named.** A creature whose name it has sung has disadvantage on saves against it. Permanently.
SPELLS: DC 19 -- at will *sanctuary*, *silence* -- 3/day *mass cure wounds*, uninvited
**Out-sing it.** DC 19 Performance ends the encounter outright. Nothing else will.
> Area 2 · arrives late, like everything in that room
:::

:::stat Aumreth's Antibody | CR 15 | AC 19 | HP 232 | SPD 40 ft | STR +8 | DEX +2 | CON +7 | INT +0 | WIS +4 | CHA -1
**Two slams** +12, 10 ft: 22 (3d10+8) bludgeoning each, and none at all if the target is prone.
**Politeness.** Will not attack a creature that has not moved since its last turn.
**Immune** poison, psychic, exhaustion. **Resists** damage from divine sources.
> Area 4 · x2 · they only start when you start
:::
:::

:::cols 2
:::stat Vess Ordeal | CR 12 | AC 17 | HP 150 | SPD 30 ft | STR -1 | DEX +2 | CON +2 | INT +7 | WIS +4 | CHA +3
SAVES: INT +12, WIS +9
SPELLS: DC 20, +12 -- at will *dispel magic*, *counterspell* -- 3/day *wall of force*, *banishment* -- 1/day *forcecage*
**Talks first, and will not kill.** Every spell is control. She fights only to keep the spike in.
> Area 5 · the fight is optional and she will say so
:::

:::puzzle The Spike | Area 5 · a decision, not a check
LOOKS: Forty feet of cold iron, a third of the way into a heart the size of a cathedral.
SOLVE: Three creatures and DC 20 Strength, or one *wish*, or Vess's consent — which she gives if someone names another way to save the towns.
FAIL: There is none. In and out are both answers. Make them say it aloud, and write down who said it.
:::
:::

:::pitch
The keeper of Lighthouse Rock has filed nothing for eleven weeks and the light has burned every single night of them. Navigation needs to know who has been lighting it, because if the answer is nobody, then the two ships on the reef are the university's problem and the university cannot afford itself.
!! THEME -- nothing out there is haunting the lighthouse. Something has taken a job at it, and is doing the job with enormous enthusiasm and no idea what it is for.
:::

:::read WHERE IT STARTS -- READ IT ON THE ROCK, WITH THE BOAT ALREADY LEAVING
The boatman puts you off and pulls straight back out -- same hour tomorrow, sea permitting. Thirty yards of rock, a tower on it, and a tide coming in that will have all the rest. The door at the base is bolted. From the inside.
:::

:::web THE WEB -- FIVE PLACES, NO ORDER
Six hours of tide and nowhere to stand. Three ways into every room, because there is no time to search one twice.
:::

:::cols 2
:::node A | The Rock | place | start
Thirty yards of it, under at high tide, and the tower is the only thing here at midnight.
-> B  The one window that opens is the keeper's, and it is nine feet up.
-> C  The only other thing above water is the oil store vent, and it is bone dry.
-> D  The light is running, on pattern, and nobody has come down that stair in eleven weeks.
:::

:::node B | The Keeper's Room | place
Heater cold for eleven weeks. Table laid for one, precisely, as if for an inspection.
-> C  The ration book is untouched. Nothing has drawn oil since March and the tanks disagree.
-> D  The log stops on the 6th and starts again in a different rhythm. Whoever kept it went up.
-> E  The table is laid the way you lay one when you know you are being watched and copied.
:::

:::node C | The Oil Store | place
Tanks full. All of them. In week eleven. Nobody has carried a drop up that stair.
-> B  Somebody's ledger is lying, and the ledger lives upstairs.
-> D  Whatever burns up there is not burning this, and the only way to know is to be present when it lights.
-> E  A film inside the tanks -- salt and something else -- and the same film is on the stair rail all the way up.
:::

:::node D | The Lamp Gallery | place
The lens, the clockwork, and the thing that has been turning it since March.
-> B  Vosk is alive up here, and the first thing he asks for is his log.
-> C  The clockwork is turned by something that never tires and has never once wanted oil.
-> E  The lens is spotless. Something polishes it nightly and the marks are not a cloth's.
:::

:::node E | The Copier | creature
No pattern of its own. Four hundred nights of somebody else's, and getting keener.
-> D  It will not leave the gallery while the lamp is turning, and it does love the lamp.
:::
:::

+++

:::secrets
1. Vosk is alive on the gallery, extremely thin, entirely lucid, and eleven weeks into a diet of gull eggs he never wants to discuss again.
2. It lights the lamp. It learned by watching him do it four hundred times through the glass, and it is very proud of itself.
3. It has started improvising. The new patterns are prettier, and they are what the ships run onto. Nobody drowned: both crews walked ashore at low tide, furious, and are now suing each other.
5. The oil has not gone down because nothing up there is burning anything at all.
6. The log's last entry is dated three weeks from today, in Vosk's hand, and Vosk has not written it yet.
:::

:::cols 2
:::box red | TROUBLE
- The tide. Six hours, thirty yards of rock, no high ground but the tower.
- It copies patterns, yours included -- marching order, watch rotations, the way you knock.
- The lamp lights at dusk whether or not anybody is ready, and Vosk will fight to go on keeping it.
:::

:::box gold | WAYS OUT
- Kill the light and take the log. The finding is all Navigation actually asked for.
- Get Vosk down. He weighs nothing now and does not want to come.
- Teach it the true pattern. It wants to be told, and nobody has told it anything in eleven weeks.
:::
:::

:::beats
1. **A better-looking pattern tonight**, and a grain barge out of Highport takes it for the old one.
2. **Pilots stop trusting every light on the coast**, which shuts Highport more thoroughly than a war would.
3. **It learns a pattern that means *come in***, and runs that one instead, helpfully, all night.
:::

:::npc Maerin Vosk | Keeper, nine years · alive, thin, and sick of gulls
WANT: The light lit correctly. That is the whole of it and has been for eleven weeks.
VOICE: Dry, courteous, hoarse. Answers everything, volunteers nothing.
BREAK: He knows what lights it. He let it. It was that or the light went out.
LINE: I never taught it. I only did the job in front of it, every night, until it could.
USE: The true pattern, and the only witness Navigation will accept.
:::

:::cols 2
:::puzzle The Pattern | the gallery · three flashes, long dark, repeat
LOOKS: The clockwork turns, the lens sweeps, and the count is wrong in a way that takes a full minute to be sure of.
SOLVE: Run the true pattern in front of it, by hand, on the shutters, until it copies that instead of its own.
FAIL: It copies the last thing it saw. Show it a waving lantern and it waves one all night, at everything afloat.
:::
